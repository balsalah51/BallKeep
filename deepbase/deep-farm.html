FAQ

How DeepBase works.

What is DeepBase?

Four baseball boards that go deeper than The Keep and The Diamond: the full dynasty tape, the full farm, majors only, and everyday lineups.

How is Deep Dynasty built?

RotoGraphs, The Dynasty Guru, and FantasyPros Dynasty ECR. Every name those three ranked. Unranked boards are skipped, never treated as 999.

How is Deep Farm different from The Farm?

The Farm is the top 100. Deep Farm keeps every prospect those five lists ranked.
