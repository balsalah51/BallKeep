Chelsea in talks with Emiliano Martínez’s camp and braced for Enzo Fernández bid

Aug 26, 2026 · 15:18 UTC · 1 source

Enzo Fernández: Experienced goalkeeper wants to leave Aston Villa Manchester City expected to push for midfielder Chelsea have been offered Emiliano Martínez and are considering making a bid for the goalkeeper. The club are in talks with Martínez’s camp and are braced for a bid from Manchester City for the World Cup winner’s Argentina teammate Enzo Fernández. Martínez has told Aston Villa he wants to leave but a move to Juventus collapsed last week after Villa had signed his replacement, Zion Suzuki , from Parma for £30m.
