Transfer roundup: Moyes wants to keep Armstrong while Forest agree Muñoz deal

Aug 29, 2026 · 18:14 UTC · 1 source

‘I don’t think we can afford to lose any players,’ says Moyes Forest’s Armstrong bid said to be £40m; Palace agree £22m fee The sale of the 19-year-old Harrison Armstrong to Nottingham Forest would be a “club decision” for Everton, according to David Moyes. After his team’s 1-1 draw at Bournemouth , in which Armstrong played the full 90 minutes, Moyes said he would prefer to keep a player for whom a reported £40m offer has been made. “I know there’s been an offer, I don’t really think there’s much else I’ve got to say about it,” Moyes said, initially refusing to be drawn.
