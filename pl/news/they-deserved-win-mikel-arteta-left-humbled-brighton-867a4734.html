‘They deserved to win’: Mikel Arteta left humbled by Brighton defeat

Sep 19, 2026 · 17:34 UTC · 1 source

Charalampos Kostoulas and Pascal Groß: Arsenal’s 100% start to season comes to an end Manager says team put themselves in trouble from start Mikel Arteta admitted Arsenal were taught a lesson by free-scoring Brighton as their 100% start to the season was abruptly ended on Saturday . Not since 10 May 2023 - 1,224 days ago - when the same opponents won 3-0 at the Emirates Stadium had the Premier League champions lost by three goals. But they were second-best throughout as Pascal Gross, Charalampos Kostoulas and Chema Andrés rounded off a comprehensive victory. Board reading of Guardian Football.
