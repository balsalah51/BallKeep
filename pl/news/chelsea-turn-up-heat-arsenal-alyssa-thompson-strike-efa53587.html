Chelsea turn up heat on Arsenal as Alyssa Thompson strike settles WSL derby

Sep 27, 2026 · 21:21 UTC · 2 sources

Chelsea beat Arsenal in a feisty encounter at Stamford Bridge to continue their steady start to the Women's Super League season. Arsenal’s title chances were dealt a significant blow after a narrow defeat at the hands of Chelsea. Alyssa Thompson’s first-half finish was the difference on a competitive afternoon at Stamford Bridge but one that ultimately left Renée Slegers with more questions than answers about how to save their season.
