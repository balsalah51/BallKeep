Transfer roundup: Ollie Watkins completes move to Al-Hilal from Aston Villa

Aug 30, 2026 · 17:12 UTC · 2 sources

Ollie Watkins completes his £51m move to Saudi Pro League side Al Hilal from Aston Villa. England forward’s exit continues busy summer for Villa News follows Emiliano Martínez moving to Chelsea The England forward Ollie Watkins has joined the list of high-profile departures from Aston Villa after completing a move to the Saudi Arabian team Al-Hilal for a reported £50m. The 30-year-old has been at Villa since joining from Brentford in 2020 and reached double figures in goals every season he was at the club. Aggregated from BBC Football, Guardian Football.
