Nicolas Jackson transfer news: Villa close to £65m move for Chelsea forward as Ollie Watkins nears exit

Aug 30, 2026 · 08:44 UTC · 8 sources

Robert Sánchez and Ollie Watkins: Argentinian set to displace Sánchez as Blues’ No 1 ‘Coming to Chelsea was an easy decision to make’ Chelsea have completed the signing of the Aston Villa goalkeeper Emiliano Martínez. The 33-year-old, who has been agitating to leave Villa for the last two summers, has finally got his wish as he has moved for £7.5m, signing a three-year contract at Stamford Bridge. Martínez, who won the 2022 World Cup with Argentina and twice secured the Yashin Trophy for the best goalkeeper, is set to displace Robert Sánchez as Chelsea’s No 1 goalkeeper.
