Alexander-Arnold proves his worth to England with trademark assist against Czechia | Jacob Steinberg

Sep 29, 2026 · 22:22 UTC · 1 source

Jaidon Anthony: The full-back is one of the best passers England has produced: Thomas Tuchel must find a place for him It was the question nobody was asking: could Trent Alexander-Arnold do it against 10 game but limited Czechs on a mild Tuesday night in Prague? The answer was a resounding yes. Alexander-Arnold played well after starting for the first time under Thomas Tuchel, demonstrating his value to England by helping them work out the dreaded low block with a trademark assist for Anthony Gordon to settle the nerves in a game coloured by Czechia losing Pavel Sulc to a debatable red card midway through the Board reading of Guardian Football.
