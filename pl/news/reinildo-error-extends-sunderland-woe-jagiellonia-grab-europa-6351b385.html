Reinildo error extends Sunderland woe as Jagiellonia grab Europa League draw

Nov 26, 2026 · 22:27 UTC · 1 source

Robin Roefs and Reinildo Mandava: Jagiellonia Bialystok’s head coach, Adrian Siemieniec, claimed this trip to Wearside to face Premier League opposition represented one of the biggest highlights of a career in which he has already celebrated a Polish title. His players did everything to guarantee it was not an anticlimax as they revelled in highlighting Sunderland’s struggles in front of goal courtesy of some intelligent defending. Along the way they also responded to falling behind with an almost immediate equaliser and forced the impressive Robin Roefs into the evening’s more taxing saves. Board reading of Guardian Football.
