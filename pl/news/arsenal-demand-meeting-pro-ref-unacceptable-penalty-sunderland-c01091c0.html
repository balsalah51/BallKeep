Arsenal demand meeting with Pro Ref over ‘unacceptable’ penalty at Sunderland

Sep 14, 2026 · 12:46 UTC · 2 sources

Enzo Le Fée, David Raya Martín and Daniel Ballard: Explanation wanted after spot-kick given against them Arsenal likely to raise penalty not awarded at Aston Villa Arsenal are seeking an explanation from Pro Ref over the penalty awarded to Sunderland on Saturday that Mikel Arteta described as “ unacceptable at this level ”. It is understood the Premier League champions contacted Pro Ref on Monday y to request a meeting after Ezri Konsa was penalised during an incident involving Dan Ballard at the Stadium of Light. David Raya made a brilliant save to deny Enzo Le Fée’s spot-kick before Arsenal went on to win 2-0. Aggregated from Guardian Football, Sky Sports Football.
