Pochettino’s new-manager bounce falls flat with De Ligt move a disastrous step | Jamie Jackson

Nov 22, 2026 · 20:58 UTC · 1 source

Decision to pick the Dutch defender over Harry Maguire backfired as Manchester United fell apart in the Anfield bearpit Chaotic, bewildering, and on this evidence in a relegation dogfight: welcome to the three‑ring circus that is Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino. This ended as a 4-0 rout in favour of Liverpool, the new head coach overseeing the poorest opening result of the eight who have followed Sir Alex Ferguson since May 2013.
