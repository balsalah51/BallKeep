Liverpool hand Pochettino nightmare Manchester United start after Yoro sees red

Nov 22, 2026 · 18:35 UTC · 1 source

Dominik Szoboszlai, Alexander Isak, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Leny Yoro: Green shoots of recovery have lasted longer in a blast-furnace than in the Mauricio Pochettino era at Manchester United. On another abysmal Anfield day for the fallen giants of Manchester, Pochettino’s debut descended into an ordeal after merely eight minutes when Leny Yoro was sent off for denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity. Liverpool would not be denied anything else. Board reading of Guardian Football.
