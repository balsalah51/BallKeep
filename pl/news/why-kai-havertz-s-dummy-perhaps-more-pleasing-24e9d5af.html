Why Kai Havertz’s dummy was perhaps more pleasing than a Lionel Messi waltz | Max Rushden

Sep 10, 2026 · 13:00 UTC · 1 source

Kai Havertz: The Arsenal striker reminded us grace can exist in the non-stop pace of football with his involuntary noise-provoking act During Covid, when sport was back but behind closed doors, I hosted a number of strange online shows to fill the void of real life. The highlight was chatting to Darren Gough about a lifesize bronze rhinoceros for 45 minutes to 15 small boxes on a Zoom call. I don’t recall who it was for and where the rhinoceros came from - but it might be in his garden. Board reading of Guardian Football.
