Emery takes responsibility as Forest strike late at winless Aston Villa

Sep 12, 2026 · 16:09 UTC · 1 source

Morgan Gibbs-White, Igor Jesus and Liam Delap: This summer there were a couple of significant pieces of the puzzle for Oliver Glasner to put into place at Nottingham Forest. The first was a mobile striker to lead the line and the other was a right wing-back to fit his favoured 3-4-3 system. Enter Liam Delap and Daniel Muñoz, both of whom had a big say in Forest’s first win under Glasner, though the match-winner was one of the old brigade in Igor Jesus.
