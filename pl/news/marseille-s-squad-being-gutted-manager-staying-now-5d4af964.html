Marseille’s squad is being gutted but their manager is staying – for now

Aug 31, 2026 · 16:11 UTC · 1 source

Bruno G.: It has been a tough transfer window for Marseille and a 2-0 defeat at Monaco suggests it could be a long season By Get French Football News A humid night in Monaco provided an opportunity to gauge the temperature of the Marseille supporters. Despite the ban on away fans at the Stade Louis II, plenty were in attendance for their 2-0 defeat in the Principality. There were cheers for their new manager, Bruno Génésio, and boos for Geoffrey Kondogbia, but it was the silence for much of the second half that spoke volumes. Board reading of Guardian Football.
