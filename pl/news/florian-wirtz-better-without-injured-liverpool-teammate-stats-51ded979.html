Liverpool ‘optimistic’ Florian Wirtz available for Chelsea visit, says Andoni Iraola

Nov 24, 2026 · 19:02 UTC · 12 sources

Playmaker in best form since moving to Anfield ‘I am really enjoying playing under Iraola’ Florian Wirtz has credited Andoni Iraola with helping him recapture the form that made him one of the most coveted players in Europe when he joined Liverpool in a deal worth up to £116m in 2025. The Germany international believes he is in the best form of his Liverpool career, having had a difficult debut season under Arne Slot and also struggled in the early weeks of Iraola’s reign.
