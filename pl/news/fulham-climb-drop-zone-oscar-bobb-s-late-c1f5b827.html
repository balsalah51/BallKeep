Fulham climb out of drop zone after Oscar Bobb’s late show sinks Coventry

Oct 24, 2026 · 18:59 UTC · 2 sources

Fulham's Oscar Bobb scores in the 80th minute against Coventry to earn back-to-back victories in the Premier League. Oscar Bobb’s first Premier League goal for Fulham earned a win against Coventry that lifted Álvaro Arbeloa’s side clear of the relegation zone. Bobb produced a fine bit of individual skill to score in the 80th minute, twisting his way into the area and bending a shot into the far corner. Aggregated from BBC Football, Guardian Football.
