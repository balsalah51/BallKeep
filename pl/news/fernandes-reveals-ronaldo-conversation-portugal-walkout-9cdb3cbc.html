‘Portugal’s greatest symbol’: Bruno Fernandes backs Cristiano Ronaldo despite walkout

Oct 03, 2026 · 19:02 UTC · 3 sources

Fernandes: ‘there are no divisions in the national team’ Ronaldo left team camp last week in selection row Bruno Fernandes has said Cristiano Ronaldo remains “the greatest symbol” of the Portugal team, despite his decision to walk out on the squad this week. Ronaldo said on Wednesday he had left the team camp after the Portugal coach, Jorge Jesus, said he would not start against Denmark. Portugal went on to win the Nations League contest 4-2 without him, and Ronaldo’s international future remains in doubt. Aggregated from Guardian Football, Fernandes, Sky Sports Football.
