Bruno Fernandes transfer news: Manchester United captain not for sale at any price despite Galatasaray…

Nov 27, 2026 · 22:01 UTC · 9 sources

Club activate option and plan further talks with captain ‘I am not nice,’ says Pochettino as he targets improvement Manchester United have triggered a clause to extend the captain Bruno Fernandes’ contract until the summer of 2028 on a day when Mauricio Pochettino warned his players they should not expect him to be “nice” in his attempts to turn around the club’s fortunes. Fernandes’ deal was due to expire in June and by exercising the option United have avoided the risk from January that he could negotiate a pre-contract agreement with a club abroad to join on a free next summer.
