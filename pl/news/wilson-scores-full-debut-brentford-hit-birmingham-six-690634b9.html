Carabao Cup round-up: Leeds beat Forest again, Brentford hit Birmingham for six

Aug 25, 2026 · 22:41 UTC · 4 sources

Dan James sets Leeds on way, Nathan Collins doubles up Peterborough thump Watford 5-1, West Ham win 4-1 Leeds secured a second win at Premier League rivals Nottingham Forest in three days to book a spot in the Carabao Cup third round. Dan James’s 50th-minute opener and a Neco Williams own goal earned a 2-0 victory for Daniel Farke’s side to back up Saturday’s 1-0 league success. Before the weekend, the West Yorkshire club had not won at the City Ground since 1972. Aggregated from Guardian Football, Sky Sports Football.
