Anton Stach snatches late Leeds winner to sour Glasner’s new dawn at Forest

Aug 22, 2026 · 16:55 UTC · 3 sources

Dominik Szoboszlai, Anton Stach and Matz Sels: For Anton Stach, another sweet direct free-kick to add to the back catalogue, this one souring Oliver Glasner’s first match in charge of Nottingham Forest. Only Dominik Szoboszlai eclipsed Stach’s record of three direct free-kick goals in the Premier League last season, one of those coming against Glasner’s Crystal Palace. Stach’s latest right-foot strike earned Leeds, fresh from a near-flawless pre-season and an expensive and exciting summer in the market, a divine opening-day victory with a couple of minutes of normal time remaining.
