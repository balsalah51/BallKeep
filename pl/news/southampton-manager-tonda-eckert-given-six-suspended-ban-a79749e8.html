Southampton manager Tonda Eckert given six-week suspended ban for Spygate scandal

Oct 07, 2026 · 16:11 UTC · 3 sources

Tonda Eckert is free to continue as Southampton manager after being given a suspended ban for his role in Spygate. Eckert was charged by FA with misconduct Southampton found to have spied on three opponents Southampton’s manager, Tonda Eckert, has been given a six-week suspended ban by the Football Association for his part in the Spygate scandal that landed the club with a four-point penalty and expulsion from last season’s Championship playoffs. The outcome means Eckert can continue in the dugout, starting with Sunday’s derby at home to Portsmouth. Aggregated from BBC Football, Guardian Football, Sky Sports Football.
