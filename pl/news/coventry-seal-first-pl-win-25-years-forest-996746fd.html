Coventry seal first PL win in 25 years at Forest amid VAR controversy

Sep 19, 2026 · 20:28 UTC · 4 sources

Josh Dasilva: Jay Dasilva's strike proves the difference as Coventry earn their first Premier League win since returning to the top flight. Coventry City's win over Nottingham Forest - thanks to Jay Dasilva's goal - was a victory which was over 25 years in the making following some dark days for the Sky Blues. Coventry earned their first Premier League victory in 25 years after Jay Dasilva's winner and a controversial disallowed Nottingham Forest equaliser saw the promoted side win 1-0 at the City Ground.
