Danny Welbeck leads Chelsea past Luton but Enzo Fernández misses Carabao Cup tie

Aug 27, 2026 · 20:40 UTC · 1 source

Enzo Fernández and Danny Welbeck: Emiliano Martínez is on his way but another high-profile Argentinian is likely to be wearing a different shade of blue soon. Chelsea have dealt with their biggest weakness, satisfying Xabi Alonso’s wish for a new goalkeeper by agreeing a £7.5m deal to sign Martínez from Aston Villa, and it will surely not be long before they have put an end to the Enzo Fernández saga. Fernández was the most notable absentee as Chelsea eased into the third round of the Carabao Cup with a 2-0 win over Luton. Desk reading of Guardian Football.
