Emery insists no change of plan for Aston Villa despite Galatasaray surface

Nov 23, 2026 · 14:56 UTC · 1 source

Ian Maatsen: ‘Hopefully the pitch will be as best as possible,’ says Villa manager Turkish champions had to move game on Saturday Unai Emery has insisted he will not alter his plans for Aston Villa’s Champions League game at ­Galatasaray on Tuesday night on account of concerns about the state of the pitch. The Turkish champions hastily moved Saturday’s home fixture from Rams Park after starting emergency repairs to the surface this month. The Villa defender Ian Maatsen said he and his teammates were aware the pitch was in a “bad condition”. Board reading of Guardian Football.
