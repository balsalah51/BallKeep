Tottenham fire blanks in draw at Nottingham Forest after Williams goal ruled out

Sep 05, 2026 · 16:11 UTC · 2 sources

Morgan Gibbs-White, Jan Paul van Hecke, Neco Williams and Sandro Tonali: For the third successive league match, Tottenham failed to score and this time they did not even muster a shot on target. Excluding a 5-1 win against a Charlton B team in the Carabao Cup, their season so far reads: five goals against and none scored. For a couple of minutes Nottingham Forest thought they had added to that goals-against tally but Neco Williams was adjudged to have handled when stooping to head in on the goalline as Sandro Tonali and Jan Paul van Hecke attempted to prevent Morgan Gibbs-White’s deflected effort bobbling into the net.
