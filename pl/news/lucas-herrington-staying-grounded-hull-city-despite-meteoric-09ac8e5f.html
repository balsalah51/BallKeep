Lucas Herrington staying grounded at Hull City despite meteoric rise: ‘It’s pretty crazy’ | Joey Lynch

The 54th Australian to play in the Premier League insists he will continue working hard - and cannot wait to face Erling Haaland On the opening day of the Premier League season, with just over an hour gone in Hull City’s clash with Manchester United, Lucas Herrington got up off the bench.
