Isak and Wirtz on target as resurgent Liverpool sweep aside Club Brugge

Nov 25, 2026 · 23:19 UTC · 4 sources

Liverpool defeat Club Brugge in a clinical performance thanks to goals from Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz, bouncing back after a defeat in their last Champions League match. Liverpool, from big money individuals to the collective, are building formidable momentum. On a bitterly cold night in Belgium, Andoni Iraola’s team delivered an accomplished away display to bring Club Brugge’s impressive run to a halt and step closer to automatic qualification from the Champions League group.
