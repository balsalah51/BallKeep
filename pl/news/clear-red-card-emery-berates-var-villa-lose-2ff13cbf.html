'A clear red card' - Emery berates VAR as Villa lose late to Forest

Sep 12, 2026 · 17:36 UTC · 1 source

Aston Villa manager Unai Emery was left frustrated with the match officials after they chose to not send off Nottingham Forest's James McAtee, following a challenge on Ian Maatsen which left the defender injured and meant Villa had to play the closing stages of the match with 10 men, with Forest taking advantage, scoring late to win 2-1 at Villa Park.
