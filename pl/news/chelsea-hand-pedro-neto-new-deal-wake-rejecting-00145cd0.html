Chelsea hand Pedro Neto new deal in wake of his rejecting Manchester City

Sep 11, 2026 · 12:55 UTC · 1 source

Winger shining after summer pursuit by several clubs Chelsea felt selling Fernández to City was right move Chelsea have rewarded Pedro Neto’s strong start to the season and commitment to the club by handing the winger a new contract. The Portugal international had summer interest from several Premier League clubs including Manchester City, but indicated a desire to stay and play for Xabi Alonso. He has signed a five-year deal with the option of an extra year and is to receive a pay rise. Board reading of Guardian Football.
