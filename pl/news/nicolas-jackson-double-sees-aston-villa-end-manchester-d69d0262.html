Nicolas Jackson double sees Aston Villa end Manchester City’s perfect start

Oct 25, 2026 · 20:38 UTC · 2 sources

Gianluigi Donnarumma, Erling Haaland and Marc Guéhi: At long last, a weakness has been spotted. Finally, the 100% winning start ends for Manchester City, much to the pleasure of Manchester United, who struck late through Elisabeth Terland to claim a point in a well-contested and at times breathless Manchester derby.
