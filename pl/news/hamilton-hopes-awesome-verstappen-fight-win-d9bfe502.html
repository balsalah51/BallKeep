Hamilton hopes for 'awesome' Verstappen fight for win

Oct 03, 2026 · 12:32 UTC · 2 sources

Lewis Hamilton says it will be important for Ferrari to focus on their own race for what is expected to be a challenging Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia but admits it would be "awesome" if they have the pace to stay with polesitter Max Verstappen from the front row. Max Verstappen claimed Red Bull's first pole position of the season as he beat Lewis Hamilton in qualifying for the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia. Board reading of Sky Sports Football.
