Isak applies finishing touch as Liverpool bring Brighton’s winning run to an end

Oct 25, 2026 · 16:12 UTC · 1 source

Alexander Isak, Florian Wirtz, Pascal Groß and Conor Bradley: Brighton’s impressive run was blown off course at Anfield where Liverpool performed with an appetite and urgency at both ends of the pitch that showed Andoni Iraola’s methods taking root. It was Liverpool, deserving winners on home soil for the first time in the Premier League this season, who looked upwardly mobile. There was more evidence of the expensive trio of Florian Wirtz, Alexander Isak and Bradley Barcola feeling at home and adapting to Iraola’s demands. Board reading of Guardian Football.
