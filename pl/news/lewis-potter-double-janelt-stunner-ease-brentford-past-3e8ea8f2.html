Lewis-Potter double and Janelt stunner ease Brentford past 10-man Everton

Nov 23, 2026 · 22:31 UTC · 3 sources

Watch highlights as Brentford earn a comfortable 3-0 win against 10-man Everton in the Premier League, including a great goal by Vitaly Janelt. It was a record-breaking occasion in more ways than one. David Moyes made history for Everton, edging clear of Harry Catterick by overseeing his 593rd game in charge of the club, but calamitous defending ruined the Scot’s evening and defeat to Brentford was inevitable after James Tarkowski received his first red card in 351 Premier League appearances. Aggregated from BBC Football, Guardian Football, Sky Sports Football.
