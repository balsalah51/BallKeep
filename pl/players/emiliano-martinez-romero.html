PitchKeep Take · The Premier

Emiliano Martínez Romero is a goalkeeper for Aston Villa, age 33, £5.0m. The Premier ranks him 57th overall by splitting Sleeper BPL 2025 (#28, 189.8 points) with the consensus of 18 other boards (98.56). Last year's Sleeper line ran 71 spots ahead of the expert/FPL mix. The third list keeps some of that production bump and refuses to throw the other boards out. That is the whole point of not just republishing The Pitch.

The 2025/26 line is 32 starts, 2835 minutes, 95 saves, 7 clean sheets and 39 goals against. Official FPL scored it at 120 points (3.8 per match) with 11 bonus. Sleeper's table turned the same counting stats into 189.8. Keepers get 8 a clean sheet, 2 a save, and −2 a goal against. That is a different sport than FPL's 10-from-saves cap, and it is why this desk ranks them at all.

Underlying: 0.0 xG, 0.08 xA, 0.08 xGI. The job is shot-stopping volume in front of whatever Aston Villa is this year. 95 saves is the Sleeper case. 7 clean sheets is the FPL case. If the back line leaks, both numbers move together and this ranking goes with them. The friendliest board is FPL EP Next at 24; the coldest is FPL xGI at 342.

Market: £5.0m, 4.6% owned into the 2026/27 FPL start, 19 boards on the file. That ownership is a leftover. Either the public missed a real season or the public correctly decided the role is not repeatable. The Premier's rank is our vote. If we have him inside the first 80 and they have him at 0.4%, that is a Sleeper buy. FPL's own EP-next is 2.6. ICT 79.2.

Instruction: he is a starter in deep Sleeper and a squad/rotation piece in 10-team FPL. Pay Premier #57 money, not a leftover 2024 reputation, not waiver dust. Nearby on The Premier: Hugo Ekitiké, Cody Gakpo, Viktor Gyökeres. Versus The Pitch (Sleeper-only #28), the hybrid moves him down to #57. That move is the third list doing its job.

Age 33 is not a moral failing and it is not invisible. Youth-lean boards (The Athletic-style remap on this desk) push him down; veteran-rating boards (WhoScored, SofaScore) keep him up. The Premier averages those instincts instead of pretending a 33-year-old goalkeeper is 24. If you need a three-year window, look younger. If you need 2026/27, he just played 2835 minutes and that still counts.

You start one goalkeeper. That positional cap is why The Premier will not put a saves leader in the overall top ten unless the consensus also shows up.
