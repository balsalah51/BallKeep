PitchKeep Take · The Premier

Alisson Becker is a goalkeeper for Liverpool, age 33, £5.5m. The Premier ranks him 93rd overall by splitting Sleeper BPL 2025 (#88, 128.8 points) with the consensus of 4 other boards (85.75). Production and the industry almost agree, which is rarer than it looks on a 24-source mix. When both sides land in the same band, The Premier stops arguing and prints the number.

The 2025/26 line is 26 starts, 2340 minutes, 57 saves, 8 clean sheets and 31 goals against. Official FPL scored it at 91 points (3.5 per match) with 8 bonus. Sleeper's table turned the same counting stats into 128.8. Keepers get 8 a clean sheet, 2 a save, and −2 a goal against. That is a different sport than FPL's 10-from-saves cap, and it is why this desk ranks them at all.

Underlying: 0.0 xG, 0.03 xA, 0.03 xGI. The job is shot-stopping volume in front of whatever Liverpool is this year. 57 saves is the Sleeper case. 8 clean sheets is the FPL case. If the back line leaks, both numbers move together and this ranking goes with them. The friendliest board is FPL EP Next at 6; the coldest is FPL ICT Index at 250.

Market: £5.5m, 10 boards on the file. Price is the FPL tax, not the rank. The Premier is who produced and who the boards still believe. FPL's own EP-next is 3.3. ICT 47.8.

Instruction: deep-league starter, shallow-league watchlist. The 2025/26 line got him onto the 400. A role change gets him off it. Watch minutes, not vibes. Nearby on The Premier: Reece James, Eberechi Eze, Rúben Dias. Versus The Pitch (Sleeper-only #88), the hybrid moves him down to #93. That move is the third list doing its job.

Age 33 is not a moral failing and it is not invisible. Youth-lean boards (The Athletic-style remap on this desk) push him down; veteran-rating boards (WhoScored, SofaScore) keep him up. The Premier averages those instincts instead of pretending a 33-year-old goalkeeper is 24. If you need a three-year window, look younger. If you need 2026/27, he just played 2340 minutes and that still counts.

You start one goalkeeper. That positional cap is why The Premier will not put a saves leader in the overall top ten unless the consensus also shows up. Becker's Sleeper line (128.8) already made The Pitch. The other boards (85.75) decide how far the hybrid lets him climb. Draft him as a keeper, not as an outfielder who happens to have gloves.
