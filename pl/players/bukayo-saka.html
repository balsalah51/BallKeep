PitchKeep Take · The Premier

Bukayo Saka is the first big 'industry versus box score' test on The Premier. Sleeper BPL 2025 had him 32nd (182.2) on 7 goals and 10 assists. The other boards average 18.1 because he is still Bukayo Saka in an Arsenal shirt. Hybrid 16. We are not putting him back in the first five on brand. We are not leaving him at 32 on a down year as if the player changed nationality.

The 2025/26 line is the honest one: the returns dipped, the name did not. Ten assists keep him in the creator band. Seven goals do not keep him in the Semenyo band. The Premier's job is to split that. Sixteen is 'still a first-round FPL name, not a first-round Sleeper name.' Draft him like the first. Pay him like the second if someone is still offering 2023 prices.

Arsenal's attack has Rice, set pieces, and a centre-forward committee. Saka is no longer the only way the ball finds the box. That is the structural minus. The plus is he is still the one defenders game-plan. Consensus 18 says the experts did not resign. Sleeper 32 says the season did.

Instruction: buy the gap. If a manager has him outside the top 25 because of 7 goals, that is the trade. If a manager still wants Haaland-adjacent capital, cash it. Premier 16 is the number we will defend.

Bukayo Saka is a midfielder for Arsenal, age 24, £9.5m. The Premier ranks him 3rd overall by splitting Sleeper BPL 2025 (#32, 182.2 points) with the consensus of 13 other boards (5.77). The industry still has him 26 spots ahead of the 2025/26 Sleeper count. The Premier pays that reputation something, not everything. Brand does not outrun a down year, and a down year does not erase a player Arsenal still builds around.
