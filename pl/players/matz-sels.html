Matz Sels is a goalkeeper for Nott'm Forest, age 34, £5.0m. The Premier ranks him 112th overall by splitting Sleeper BPL 2025 (#31, 183.4 points) with the consensus of 3 other boards (100.67). Sleeper's table turned the same counting stats into 183.4. Keepers get 8 a clean sheet, 2 a save, and −2 a goal against.
