PitchKeep Take · The Premier

Ollie Watkins is the veteran No. 9 The Premier refuses to bury. Sixteen goals, four assists, Sleeper 26, consensus 21.1, hybrid 14. Igor Thiago and João Pedro both outrank him. Haaland lapped him. He is still the fourth forward on this list because 16 goals is 16 goals and Villa still play him as the point.

The kids have the narrative. Watkins has the career. The third list is where those two sentences have to share a ranking. Fourteen is 'you can still start him, you should not still draft him like 2023.' If someone in your league missed the memo that Thiago scored 22, Watkins is the name they will overpay. Let them.

Instruction: hold in win-now FPL. Sell in a long keeper league if the offer is a younger 15-goal forward plus. Do not fade him to waivers because Semenyo plays for City now. There is still a first XI on this desk that has Watkins in it. It just no longer has him in the first six.
