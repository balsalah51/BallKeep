PitchKeep Take · The Premier

Harry Maguire is a defender for Man Utd, age 33, £5.0m. The Premier ranks him 127th overall by splitting Sleeper BPL 2025 (#158, 86.6 points) with the consensus of 5 other boards (118.4). The industry still has him 40 spots ahead of the 2025/26 Sleeper count. The Premier pays that reputation something, not everything. Brand does not outrun a down year, and a down year does not erase a player Man Utd still builds around.

The 2025/26 Premier League line is 19 starts and 1649 minutes, 1 goals and 2 assists, 7 clean sheets, 20 goals against, 22 tackles and 129 CBI. Official FPL scored it at 90 points (3.9 per match) with 5 bonus. Sleeper's table turned the same counting stats into 86.6. Defenders get 10 a goal, 7 an assist, 6 a clean sheet, and −2 a goal against. CBI at 0.4 and tackles at 1 are how a centre-back cracks the overall 50.

Underlying: 1.12 xG, 0.5 xA, 1.62 xGI and 1.5 above expected assists. The defensive events are the floor: 22 tackles, 129 clearances/blocks/interceptions, 7 shutouts. Sleeper is built to notice that. ICT and xGI boards are not, which is why a centre-back's spread on this board is usually ugly and still informative. The friendliest board is Yahoo GW1 XI at 3; the coldest is RotoWire FPL 400 at 283.

Market: £5.0m, 11 boards on the file. Price is the FPL tax, not the rank. The Premier is who produced and who the boards still believe. FPL's own EP-next is 2.2. ICT 63.0.

Instruction: he is on the 400, which is already a statement, and he is not a star. Stash in Sleeper if the minutes are real. Do not let a Pitch screenshot make you start him over a Premier top-80. Nearby on The Premier: Đorđe Petrović, Viktor Gyökeres, Dean Henderson. Versus The Pitch (Sleeper-only #158), the hybrid moves him up to #127. That move is the third list doing its job.

Age 33 is not a moral failing and it is not invisible. Youth-lean boards (The Athletic-style remap on this board) push him down; veteran-rating boards (WhoScored, SofaScore) keep him up. The Premier averages those instincts instead of pretending a 33-year-old defender is 24. If you need a three-year window, look younger. If you need 2026/27, he just played 1649 minutes and that still counts.

Full-backs and centre-backs separate on this board by attacking events. 1 goals and 2 assists are how a defender outruns his GA column. Without those, you are betting 7 clean sheets and the CBI stream (129) against 20 goals against at −2 a piece. Man Utd's 2026/27 defensive shape is therefore half of this player's rank. The other half already happened, across 19 starts.
