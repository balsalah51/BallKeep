David Raya Martín is Sleeper's reminder that goalkeepers are real. 233.8 points, Pitch #7, because saves pay 2 and clean sheets pay 8 and Arsenal stacked both. The other boards average 43.4 because FPL culture still treats keepers as the last pick in a draft. The Premier splits it to 17. That is 'he produced like a top-ten player and you still only start one.'

The 2025/26 case is the same as Gabriel's with a different multiplier. Arsenal clean sheets made two men rich on this desk. Raya got the 8s. Gabriel got the 6s plus goals. Hybrid prefers the defender who also scores, which is why Gabriel is 4 and Raya is 17. Fair.

Instruction: in Sleeper he is a first-round keeper. On The Premier he is a late second. Do not take him over Semenyo. Do take him over a 6.0 midfielder you 'kind of like.' Kelleher outscored him on The Pitch and still sits 29th here because Brentford's name does not move consensus. Raya's does, a little. That is the Arsenal tax, and for once it is earned.
