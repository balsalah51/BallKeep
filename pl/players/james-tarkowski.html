Official FPL scored it at 170 points (4.6 per match) with 12 bonus. Sleeper's table turned the same counting stats into 176.0. Defenders get 10 a goal, 7 an assist, 6 a clean sheet, and −2 a goal against. CBI at 0.4 and tackles at 1 are how a centre-back cracks the overall 50.

Underlying: 2.53 xG, 2.1 xA, 4.63 xGI. The defensive events are the floor: 51 tackles, 325 clearances/blocks/interceptions, 11 shutouts. Sleeper is built to notice that. ICT and xGI boards are not, which is why a centre-back's spread on this desk is usually ugly and still informative. The friendliest board is SofaScore mix at 5; the coldest is FPL xGI at 109.

Market: £6.0m, 9.3% owned into the 2026/27 FPL start, 18 boards on the file. Mid-range ownership means the name is known and not fully paid. That is usually where this desk lives — not the 70% tax, not the 0.2% dart. FPL's own EP-next is 2.8. ICT 165.7.

Instruction: he is a starter in deep Sleeper and a squad/rotation piece in 10-team FPL. Pay Premier #26 money, not a leftover 2024 reputation, not waiver dust. Nearby on The Premier: Adrien Truffert, Enzo Fernández, Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall. Versus The Pitch (Sleeper-only #41), the hybrid moves him up to #26. That move is the third list doing its job.

Age 33 is not a moral failing and it is not invisible. Youth-lean boards (The Athletic-style remap on this desk) push him down; veteran-rating boards (WhoScored, SofaScore) keep him up. The Premier averages those instincts instead of pretending a 33-year-old defender is 24. If you need a three-year window, look younger.
