Gianluigi Donnarumma is a goalkeeper for Man City, age 27, £5.4m. The Premier ranks him 81st overall by splitting Sleeper BPL 2025 (#69, 49.0 points) with the consensus of 4 other boards (72.75). Production and the industry almost agree, which is rarer than it looks on a 24-source mix. When both sides land in the same band, The Premier stops arguing and prints the number.

The 2025/26 line is 11 starts, 990 minutes, 28 saves, 3 clean sheets and 14 goals against. Official FPL scored it at 35 points (3.2 per match) with 1 bonus. Sleeper's table turned the same counting stats into 49.0. Keepers get 8 a clean sheet, 2 a save, and −2 a goal against. That is a different sport than FPL's 10-from-saves cap, and it is why this board ranks them at all.

The job is shot-stopping volume in front of whatever Man City is this year. 28 saves is the Sleeper case. 3 clean sheets is the FPL case. If the back line leaks, both numbers move together and this ranking goes with them. The friendliest board is RotoWire FPL 400 at 26; the coldest is FPL ICT Index at 216.

Market: £5.4m, 10 boards on the file. Price is the FPL tax, not the rank. The Premier is who produced and who the boards still believe. FPL's own EP-next is 1.7. ICT 22.5.

Instruction: deep-league starter, shallow-league watchlist. The 2025/26 line got him onto the 400. A role change gets him off it. Watch minutes, not vibes. He is 27, which on this board is neither a dart nor a warning label. Prime-age goalkeepers get ranked on what they just did and whether Man City still gives them the ball. 990 minutes says yes. The 2026/27 FPL price (£5.4m) is the app's guess at whether that continues. The Premier is the blend of that guess and the box score.
