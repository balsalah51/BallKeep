How does the PitchKeep calculator work?

Rank becomes BK Value. Rank 1 is 12,000. Fair is within 8%. Premier uses the 2026/27 list. Pitch uses Sleeper.

Which calc should I use?

Premier for 2026/27 drafts. Pitch when the deal is last season's Sleeper points. Position calcs when the trade is all forwards, mids, defenders, or keepers.
