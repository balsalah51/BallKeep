How Week 9 TE is built.

How is the weekly board built?

Super Aggregate of every Week N board that ranked the name: 50% FantasyPros ECR, 50% every other board that ranked the name. That is FantasyPros projected points, 4for4, and RotoWire when that feed has points.

What is the Proj column?

FantasyPros projected points for this week when RotoWire's feed has no skill points. RotoWire PPR points, via Sleeper, when that feed publishes them. Blank means both skipped the name.

When does this refresh?

A Monday and Tuesday job re-scrapes the public boards and rebuilds the pages.
