Two clocks · September 24, 2026

The Split

Every other site gives you one number. Dynasty rooms live on two clocks at once: the years you paid for on The Keep, and the Sundays you have to start on The Board. The Split prints both, prices both, and then lets you build a room so you can see which year that room is actually trying to win. 200 names sit on both boards. 98 lean Sunday. 52 lean years.

Your room

Put names on both clocks.

Search a name. Add it to the room. The Keep total is dynasty money. The Board total is this-year money. The window year is the sentence those two clocks write together.

    FAQ

    How The Split is built.

    What is The Split?

    Two clocks on every name. The Keep is Superflex dynasty. The Board is redraft PPR. The gap is the argument. The room tool turns a set of names into a window year.

    What is a window year?

    2026 means the room is paying for this season. 2028 means the room is paying for years. 2027 sits in the middle. Age and the two BK Values decide it.

    What does a plus gap mean?

    Keep rank minus Board rank. A plus gap is a Sunday name: higher on The Board than on The Keep. A minus gap is a years name.

    Is this a trade calculator?

    The trade calculators price one clock. The Split prices both clocks at once and then tells you which year the room is built for.
