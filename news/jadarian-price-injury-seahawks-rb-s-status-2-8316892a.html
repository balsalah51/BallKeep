Jadarian Price injury update: Latest news on Seahawks RB's status in Week 2 game

Aggregate

Seahawks rookie running back Jadarian Price left Sunday's win over the Cardinals with an injury, but it doesn't look like he is facing an extended absence from the lineup. Coach Mike Macdonald said Monday that Price suffered a shoulder injury during Sunday's win at Arizona, but "it's intact and nothing serious, it seems like," Brady Henderson of ESPN.com reports.
