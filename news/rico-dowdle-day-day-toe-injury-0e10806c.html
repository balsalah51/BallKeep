Steelers get positive injury update on Rico Dowdle

Aggregate

Steelers get positive update on Rico Dowdle. Steelers running back Rico Dowdle was able to return to Sunday's loss to the Patriots after being called questionable to return from a toe injury in the first half, but he was ultimately ruled out of the game. Dowdle "checked out relatively OK" after undergoing testing on his toe injury Tuesday and is considered day-to-day, Ian Rapoport of NFL Network reports. Aggregated from SB Nation, ProFootball Talk on NBC Sports, PFT feed, RotoWire NFL, x.com.
