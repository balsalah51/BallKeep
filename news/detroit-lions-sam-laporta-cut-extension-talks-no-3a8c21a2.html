Detroit Lions, Sam LaPorta cut extension talks with no new contract

Aggregate

The Detroit Lions and TE Sam LaPorta have reportedly cut off contract extension talks with no new deal. The chances of tight end Sam LaPorta hitting free agency next year are reportedly looking a little better. LaPorta and the Lions have broken off contract talks after several weeks of discussions, and the tight end now is set to be an unrestricted free agent in 2027, Ian Rapoport of NFL Network reports.
