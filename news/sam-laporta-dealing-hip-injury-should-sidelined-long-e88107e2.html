Sam LaPorta dealing with hip injury, but should not be sidelined for long

Aggregate

Detroit Lions tight end Sam LaPorta could miss the start of the season with a new hip injury. The Lions have an injury concern with one of their key offensive players that the club is hopeful will not linger. LaPorta is dealing with a hip injury, and head coach Dan Campbell wouldn't commit Thursday to the tight end being available for Week 1 against the Saints, Tim Twentyman of the Lions' official site reports.
