How the football wire works.

Where does BK News come from?

Hourly clusters from league RSS, Google News, X, and YouTube. Each story is an aggregate with links back to the original boards and to Keep player files.

How often does the wire refresh?

The scrape runs every hour. New URLs merge into the cluster; the static pages rebuild when the tape moved.

Why do stories link to player pages?

Named Keep, Board, and rookie files sit on the cluster so you can jump from the injury to the rank.
