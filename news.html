FAQ

How the football wire works.

Where does BK News come from?

Clusters from league RSS, Google News, X, and YouTube. Each story is an aggregate with short snippets and links out to the original boards, plus Keep player files.

How often does the wire refresh?

A GitHub Action scrapes on a schedule and writes the hub. The banner on this page says how many hours ago that last succeeded. Older than a day is marked stale.

Why do stories link to player pages?

Named Keep, Board, and rookie files sit on the cluster so you can jump from the injury to the rank.
