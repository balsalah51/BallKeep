FAQ

How BaseKeep works.

What is BaseKeep?

BaseKeep is baseball on Ball Keep. The Keep is dynasty overall, the full ranked tape, from 3 independently sourced boards (RotoGraphs, The Dynasty Guru, FantasyPros). The Diamond is this-year redraft. The Farm is the top 100 prospects. Same BK Value curve as football.

How is The Keep ranked?

Average of every source that ranked the player. Two of the boards run 500 names long.

Where is the MLB news?

BK News on this board clusters IL, roster, DFA, and manager tape. The banner on the news page says when the last scrape landed.
