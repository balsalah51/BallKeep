FAQ

How DeepBase works.

What is DeepBase?

Four baseball boards that go deeper than The Keep and The Diamond: the full dynasty tape, the full farm, majors only, and 2027 projected lineups.

Who is a farm name?

Career regular-season MLB hitting at-bats of 130 or fewer AND pitching innings of 50 or fewer. A two-way player must clear both. Stats from the MLB Stats API through 2026.

Who is a major-league name?

More than 130 career AB or more than 50 career IP. Ohtani and other two-way names sit here. Unmatched IDs are withheld, not guessed onto Majors.

How is Deep Dynasty built?

RotoGraphs, The Dynasty Guru, and FantasyPros Dynasty ECR, then every eligible farm and majors name. BK Value uses the same rank-1 = 12,000 curve.
