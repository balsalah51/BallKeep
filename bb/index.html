What This Desk Is

Keep the guys who still mash in 2030.

Redraft baseball is this summer's counting stats — you stream arms, you chase saves, you survive September. Dynasty baseball prices the next five years: peak age, prospect lists, and whether a 22-year-old shortstop is still a shortstop in 2030.

The Keep is an overall dynasty top 400 aggregated from 23 public boards, led by RotoGraphs' Aug 14 discounted-dollar model and The Dynasty Guru's Aug 10 points Top 500. The Lineup strips the pitchers (every hitter we can pin, up to 400). BK's Pitchers is the other half (150). The bullpen is two sports: saves, then saves-plus-holds.
