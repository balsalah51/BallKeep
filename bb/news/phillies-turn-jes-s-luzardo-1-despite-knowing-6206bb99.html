Phillies turn to Jesús Luzardo in Game 1 despite not knowing ‘what to expect'

Aggregate

Jesús Luzardo, Andrew Painter and Luis Arraez: After a four-pitch return, Luzardo will open against Atlanta, with Andrew Painter waiting in the wings and Luis Arraez off the Wild Card roster. Phillies interim manager Don Mattingly said Monday that Luzardo will be an opener for Game 1 of the American League Wild Card Series in Atlanta on Tuesday, Todd Zolecki of MLB.com reports.
