Emmet Sheehan wins Pacific Coast League pitcher of week

Aggregate

Emmet Sheehan is on a nice run since getting optioned to Triple-A Oklahoma City, and on Monday was named Pacific Coast League pitcher of the week after a dominant start in Sugar Land, Texas. Sheehan struck out nine in six scoreless innings on Sunday in the series finale against the Houston Astros’ Triple-A affiliate. Desk reading of SB Nation.
