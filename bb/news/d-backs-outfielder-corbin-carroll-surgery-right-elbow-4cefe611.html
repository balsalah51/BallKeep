D-backs’ outfielder Corbin Carroll has surgery on right elbow, should be ready for spring training

Aggregate

Corbin Carroll had surgery on his right elbow this week and is expected to be ready for the start of spring training in February, the Diamondbacks said. The team confirmed the surgery on Friday. A full recovery is expected to take about eight weeks. Aggregated from ESPN MLB, NBC Sports, RotoWire MLB.
