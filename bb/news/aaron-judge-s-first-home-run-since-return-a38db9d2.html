Aaron Judge's first home run since return from injured list sparks Yankees' postseason-clinching win at Twins

Aggregate

Aaron Judge and Will Warren: Judge had been 3 for 20 with 11 strikeouts since returning last week after missing 84 games because of a fractured rib. Aaron Judge hit his first home run since May 24, Will Warren did not allow an earned run and the New York Yankees used a six-run eighth inning to beat the Minnesota Twins in Monday's series opener. The Yankees clinched their ninth playoff berth in 10 seasons - and 27th in the past 32 seasons - thanks to the Blue Jays' 6-5 loss to the Tigers. Aggregated from NBC Sports, SNY, USA TODAY Sports.
