Jamie Arnold injury update: Scary moment leaves prospect with facial fractures

Aggregate

Athletics prospect Jamie Arnold escaped serious injury after being hit in the face. Arnold has been diagnosed with multiple fractures on the left side of his face as well a corneal abrasion after being struck by a line drive during a start Tuesday at Triple-A Las Vegas, according to the pitcher's mother.
