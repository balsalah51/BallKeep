Will Smith finishes rehab week with Triple-A Oklahoma City

Aggregate

Dodgers catcher Will Smith completed his minor league rehab assignment on Sunday night, catching six innings for Triple-A Oklahoma City and collected one single in three at-bats. In all, Smith played five games for the Comets last week - four starts and 25 innings behind the plate, and once as designated hitter. Board reading of SB Nation.
