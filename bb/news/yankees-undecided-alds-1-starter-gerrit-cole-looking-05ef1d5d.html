Yankees undecided on ALDS Game 1 starter, but Gerrit Cole looking forward to his next turn

Aggregate

The Yankees had the luxury of picking between the two after sweeping the Red Sox in the AL wild-card series with Cam Schlittler and Max Fried. Following their Wild Card Series win, Yankees manager Aaron Boone was undecided on an ALDS Game 1 starter, but said it's between Gerrit Cole and Carlos Rodon. The Yankees are going the expected route, starting Gerrit Cole in Game 1 of the ALDS against the Rays on Saturday night in Tampa Bay.
