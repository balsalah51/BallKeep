The tape

Colston Loveland is the 2025 tight-end prospect The Keep kept in the 30s because the position is barren and the tape is not. Average 35.67, KTC a bit hotter at 29. The Bears 2025 reel is Loveland as a move tight end: seams, choice routes, a young passer who can use him as a security blanket.

In Superflex you draft tight ends when the board gets silly, not when you are still staring at Maye. Loveland is a third-round startup name, a second-round pick in TE-premium, and a hold in every dynasty that already missed Bowers and McBride.

Plus

  • Keep #35 — 2025 Bears tight end with receiving chops next to Williams and Odunze.
  • Youth at TE is scarce; KTC having him 29th is the market paying for that scarcity.

Minus

  • Yates 35th is a TE2/3 in redraft until the target share is undeniable.
  • Chicago's passing pie is crowded (Odunze, Burden, the backfield).
