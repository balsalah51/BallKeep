The tape

Jayden Daniels is the Superflex argument with the widest expert spread on our sheet. Erickson has him second overall. Brown has him 23rd. Everyone else is in the 6-17 band. Ball Keep averages that into Keep #8, which is our way of saying: we believe the rushing quarterback is a top-10 SF asset, and we are not pretending one cold ranking doesn't exist.

The 2025 deep ball to McLaurin we clipped is the passing-game proof people wanted after the rookie year. Washington can still be a messy supporting-cast week, but the quarterback creating explosives with his legs and his arm is the profile that wins Superflex titles. Maye is the cleaner 'pocket plus' bet for some; Daniels is the 'he can beat you two ways every week' bet.

Plus

  • Keep #8, seven sources, dual-threat SF QB2/3 on most expert lists.
  • Erickson has him SF #2 - the loudest young-QB bet besides Maye.

Minus

  • Derek Brown slamming him to 23 is the biggest expert split on The Keep.
  • Yates redraft ranking in the 40s: 1QB leagues do not have to roster the rushing floor the same way.
  • Cold #9: Left elbow again. Mariota just won in Seattle. ESPN already talks about a month. Superflex rooms that just watched 1-2 will sell a Keep quarterback cheap. Pay the years, sit the month. - ESPN, FantasyPros
