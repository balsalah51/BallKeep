The tape

Nick Singleton is a Penn State running back with a 2025 clip against Nevada that shows the burst, parked on our board as a mid-second committee back in Tennessee. That sentence has three hedges and one compliment. The compliment is the athlete. The hedges are the job.

Cam Ward and Carnell Tate are the Titans' passing-game future. Singleton is a rushing-game dart. If the committee thins, he is a hit at 2.06. If it doesn't, he is a taxi-squad name you forget until Week 12.

Plus

  • Rookie #15 - mid-second committee back, Penn State 2025 vs. Nevada clip, Titans landing spot.
  • Tennessee will run; a piece of that pie can become an RB3.

Minus

  • Our note: mid-second committee back - the scariest words in a rookie draft besides 'fullback.'
  • Titans backfield can be a three-man mess even after Ward/Tate arrive.
