The tape

Puka Nacua's 2025 snow-angle catch is the player in one clip: body control, contested-catch strength, and a quarterback who trusts him when the play dies. Ball Keep has him seventh in Superflex. Yates has him as a top-five PPR pick. That is a receiver who skipped the 'prove it again' year and went straight to alpha usage.

The Keep's source mix loves him because the production is not scheme-dependent gadgetry. He wins inside and outside. He wins in weather. He wins when the Rams need a chunk on third down. Among non-QBs only Chase, Bijan, JSN, and Gibbs are consistently ranked ahead of him on our boards.
