The tape

CeeDee Lamb is still a Keep top-20 asset, and the 65-yard touchdown clip from 2025 is the reminder: when Dallas needs a play, they still call his number. Yates has him as a top-13 PPR pick. Dynasty Nerds 14th. KTC 19th. That is a WR1 who slipped a tier, not a WR who fell off the board.

The conversation is no longer 'is Lamb a 1.01 receiver?' It is 'is Lamb still a first-round Superflex skill player now that JSN, Nacua, and Nabers have taken the young-alpha seats?' Our answer is yes, with a lower ceiling rank. You draft him as a WR1. You do not need him to be Chase.

Plus

  • Keep #16, Yates PPR WR1/WR2 range - Dallas still funnels money through Lamb.
  • Three-board average of 15 with no real 'he's done' camp on the long sheets.

Minus

  • Cowboys quarterback and scheme noise is the 2026 tax after years of being a locked WR1.
  • Not on the short X expert slices we captured - some SF lists moved on to younger names.
