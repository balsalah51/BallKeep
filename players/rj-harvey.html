The tape

RJ Harvey is Cold because the job died, not because the player did. Draft Sharks: Broncos brought J.K. Dobbins back, not a locked lead back. The 2025 three-TD game against Dallas is why people paid. The depth chart is why they should sell. Jonah Coleman being a Denver rookie dart on our board is extra fog.

If you can still get RB2 money, take it. If the market has already crushed Harvey to RB4, you hold the youth and wait for 2027. Superflex rebuilders can live with a committee back who can score three times in a game. Superflex contenders cannot start a committee back and also feel clever.

Plus

  • 2025 Broncos 3-TD Week 8 reel is a real scoring-back week; the talent to finish is there.
  • Youth; if Dobbins is a short-term rental, Harvey is the 2027 back.

Minus

  • Cold: Draft Sharks - Broncos brought J.K. Dobbins back; not a locked lead back.
  • Denver committees plus Dobbins is how rookie-back ADPs die.
