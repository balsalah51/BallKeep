Ball Keep take

Denzel Boston's 2025 Washington vs. Ohio State tape is a receiver who can win in a big-game environment. Our rookie board has him 13th, early/mid second, to Cleveland. That landing spot is opportunity and danger in the same sentence, which is also how we described Concepcion.

If you already took a Browns WR, you do not need two. If you didn't, Boston is a fine second-round dart behind the real first-round receivers. Superflex depth is a real thing. Starting him in Week 1 is not, unless the depth chart is on fire.

Pay a second. Do not pay a first. Do not write a poem about it. Take the player if the board is dry.
