Ball Keep Take

TreVeyon Henderson is a Hot process pick on a good young offense. Sports Arena: buy for a projected late 2027 first, youth plus the Patriots backfield. That is a price note. The 2025 electric reel is a player who can take a screen to the house, which is exactly what Drake Maye's offense wants in a back.

The minus is New England's habit of using two backs until one of them forces the issue. Henderson can force the issue. He might also be the change-of-pace forever. Superflex rebuilders should be happy to hold a late-2027-first-priced back with this tape. Contenders need the volume to print before they start him every week.

If the offer is a late 2027 first, Sports Arena says you can buy. We agree. If the offer is Jeanty, you are in the wrong conversation.
