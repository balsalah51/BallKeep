The tape

D'Andre Swift's 175-yard game against Washington is the redraft plus. Yates 47th, standard 42nd. When Swift gets a full-game diet, he looks like an RB2. When he doesn't, he looks like the committee back everyone drafted him as. Chicago will keep doing both.

Superflex dynasty has no reason to pay for this profile. Young backs exist. Swift's useful years are now. If you are contending, you start him when the snap share is right. If you are rebuilding, you do not have Swift. If you do, you sell him to a Bears stacker.

Plus
