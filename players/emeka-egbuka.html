The tape

Emeka Egbuka is the 2025 rookie receiver The Keep parked in the 29 hole — the 'we believe the tape' spot, not the 'we believe the ADP' spot. Three boards, average 29.33. The Weeks 1-4 Buccaneers reel is a player winning at the catch point and threatening the paint, which is what you want from a first-year wideout who is supposed to grow into WR2 money.

Tampa Bay will keep throwing, and that is the plus. The minus is that rookie receiver rankings in August 2026 are still part projection. Redraft has him in the 30s, which is a WR3 with upside. Dynasty has him inside the top 30 Superflex names, which is a bet on 2027-2028.

Plus

  • Keep #29 — 2025 Buccaneers rookie WR tape with real red-zone juice.
  • Youth in an offense that still throws enough to support a WR2/WR3 who can become more.

Minus

  • Yates 33rd: 2026 is still a crowded Tampa bay (Adams on the Cold board as a Ram, but TB has its own mouths).
  • Rookie-year highlights can be a 12-game sample pretending to be a career.
