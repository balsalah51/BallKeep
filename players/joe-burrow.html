The tape

Joe Burrow at Keep #15 is Ball Keep admitting two true things at once. One: the passing talent and the Chase pairing are still SF QB1 peak. Two: you cannot rank him with Maye and Allen until the availability question is boring again. PFN, Nerds, and KTC keep him inside the top 20. That is respect, not a blanket.

The 2025 highlight pack is Burrow when the timing is right: anticipation throws, Chase in stride, the Bengals looking like a Superflex cheat code. The weeks he is not out there are the weeks Cincinnati managers start scrolling waiver QBs and wondering why they paid 1.01 prices two summers ago.

Plus

  • Keep #15 — when healthy, a top-five SF passing week with Chase and Higgins.
  • Dynasty Nerds and KTC still treat him as a franchise QB1, not a rebound dart.

Minus

  • Availability is the entire minus; 2025 highlighted how fast the Bengals offense breaks without him.
  • Missing Yates' top 50 is the redraft market saying it will not pay for a hope-he's-right QB1.
