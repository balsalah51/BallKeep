The tape

Chris Bell's 2025 Louisville vs. Miami tape is a receiver who can win in space, now actually in Miami as a Dolphin. Our board has him 17th, mid/late second. Speed in that offense is not a new idea. It is still a useful idea if the snaps are there.

The minus is the snaps. Miami can be a committee of speed and a quarterback question mark. Bell is a stash. If the depth chart opens, we will move him up. Until then, he is a name on a page with a clip.

Plus

  • Rookie #17 - mid/late second, Louisville 2025 vs. Miami clip, Dolphins WR dart.
  • Miami can still create explosives for a speed WR even in a messy year.

Minus

  • Dolphins WR room and quarterback questions are a weekly void risk.
  • Mid/late-second WRs in Miami have a habit of becoming preseason stars and September inactives.
