Ball Keep Take

Brock Bowers is the tight end The Keep is willing to treat like a receiver. KeepTradeCut has him eighth overall in Superflex. Dynasty Nerds 11th. PFN 21st. Average 13.33, Keep #11. That is a 1.01-at-the-position price, and the 2025 Raiders highlight reel is why: motions, option routes, YAC, and a target share tight ends are not supposed to hold.

Redraft PPR agrees more than Superflex does. Yates has him inside the top 20 overall, which is TE-premium scoring in practice even before your league adds extra tight-end points. If you play with a TE premium, Bowers is a first-rounder without the debate. If you play Superflex, you are choosing him over a QB2, and that is the only reason he is not top eight on our sheet.

Las Vegas will keep being a noisy offense. That is the minus. It has not stopped Bowers from being the youngest 'he is the offense' tight end since Kelce's prime. We would rather be long Bowers than long a 28-year-old WR2 with a prettier quarterback.

Brock Bowers is #20 on The Keep, Ball Keep's Superflex dynasty keystone. The number is the average of every board that ranked him (18.2 across 5 sources). He is a TE for LV. BK Value on this slot is 5,115, the same decaying curve as baseball and PitchKeep: 12,000 at 1.01.
