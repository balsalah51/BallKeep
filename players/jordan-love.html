The tape

Jordan Love is the Superflex QB2 who just got his receivers back in the conversation. Keep #41. The 65-yard Can't-Miss bomb is the plus: when Love has time, the ball comes out on a rope and Green Bay can score from anywhere. Watson sitting on our Hot board (route share, departed competition) and Kraft's positive ACL news are not nothing. They are the 2026 stack.

He is not climbing into the Daniels/Herbert band on our sheet without a full season of QB1 finishes. He is also not falling into streaming range. That middle is valuable in Superflex. It is boring on Twitter.
