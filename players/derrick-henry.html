Ball Keep Take

Derrick Henry is a redraft RB1 and a dynasty countdown clock. Yates 11th, our standard board eighth, The Keep off the top 50. The 44-yard Can't-Miss race is Henry in 2025: still faster than linebackers who should have learned. Ravens football still wants to hand him the ball until the math stops working.

Do not get cute and fade him in 2026 redraft because of a tweet about age. Do get cute and fade him in dynasty startups because of the same tweet. We contain multitudes. Henry contains miles.
