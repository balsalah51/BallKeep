Ball Keep Take

Bo Nix is the Superflex QB2 who became a starter you can live with. Keep #34, KTC 31st, Nerds 35th. The 2025 Can't-Miss 43-yard strike to Sutton is the plus: Denver can push the ball, Nix can take the shot, the Broncos are not a dink-and-dunk apology every week.

He is not Maye. He is not Daniels. He is the player you draft when those two are gone and you still need a second Superflex starter who will be in the same offense next year. That is valuable. It is not sexy. Sexy is how people end up with a rookie QB3 and no floor.

The minus is the ceiling. If your league mates are taking Nix over Herbert or Purdy because of one viral throw, fade that. If they are taking a 34-year-old streaming QB over Nix, buy. Keep #34 is a starter ranking with a small amount of juice, not a 1.01 in disguise.
