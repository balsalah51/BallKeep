The tape

Amon-Ra St. Brown is the receiver you start every week and only notice when he sits. Keep #14, Yates PPR ninth, KTC ninth. Detroit's offense manufactures touches for him the way it manufactures explosives for Gibbs. The 2025 touchdown reel is not one circus catch; it is a pile of won routes.

Ball Keep's long boards still have him behind the Chase/JSN/Nacua cluster, which is a ceiling ranking, not a respect ranking. St. Brown's floor is the reason he survives Superflex rooms that otherwise only want quarterbacks and a single sexy WR. He is the glue.

Plus

  • Keep #14 and Yates PPR top 10 — the most reliable target in Detroit.
  • Route precision and slot/alpha hybrid usage travel even if Gibbs takes rushing headlines.

Minus

  • Only three Keep sources; the ceiling vs. Chase/JSN/Nacua is the debate, not the floor.
  • WR1 price with a WR2-in-SF-startups outcome if you need a true 1.01 receiver.
