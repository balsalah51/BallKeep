Jaxson Dart at Keep #32 is a Superflex bet on the Giants finally having a quarterback worth stacking with Nabers. Three long boards, average 30.33. The 2025 season highlights are flashes: arm talent, designed movement, a rookie/second-year passer looking like he belongs on an NFL field rather than a hope-and-a-prayer field.

That is enough for dynasty. It is not enough for Yates. Superflex managers should treat Dart as a hold/buy if the rest of your QBs are aging. Redraft managers should treat him as a bench dart until the weekly floor is a QB2 you can start without a prayer.

The minus is the franchise. The plus is the age. We have him in the Bo Nix/Jordan Love cluster of 'starting QB who might still climb,' not in the Maye cluster of 'already climbed.' Rank him with honest uncertainty, not with a meme.
