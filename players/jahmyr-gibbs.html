The tape

Jahmyr Gibbs is the running back the trade market will not stop paying for. KeepTradeCut has him as the Superflex 1.01, ahead of Chase and Bijan. Yates has him as the PPR 1.01. Ball Keep's seven-board mix lands him sixth because a few expert lists still stuff quarterbacks in front of every back. That gap is a buying opportunity in startups that use KTC as gospel and a selling opportunity if someone offers you Allen/Maye straight up in Superflex.

The 2025 Eagles game we embedded — 146 yards of burst and cutback — is why scouts stopped calling him a change-of-pace. He is a feature back who happens to be explosive. Detroit will still sprinkle Montgomery into short-yardage, and that is the minus you live with. It has not stopped Gibbs from being a weekly RB1.

Plus

  • KeepTradeCut SF #1 and Yates PPR/STD RB1 — the market's favorite young back.
  • Detroit's scheme still manufactures explosive touches; 2025 film vs. Philadelphia is the advertisement.

Minus

  • Some Superflex experts (Erickson 16) will take two passers and a WR before Gibbs.
  • Committee noise with David Montgomery never fully goes away in Detroit.
