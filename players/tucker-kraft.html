The tape

Tucker Kraft is a Hot name because the medicals turned and the talent was already TE1. FantasyPros: positive ACL reports, 25 years old, looked top-four at the position before the injury. The 2025 Packers reel is Kraft as a receiver who hurts linebackers in space. That player is a dynasty TE1 if the knee is the 2024 player again.

The minus is every ACL: the first six games can look like rust even when the MRI looks like a victory lap. Superflex TE-premium teams should buy. Redraft should fade the first month and buy the dip if the rust is real. Dynasty should not sell for a late second because of a limp in August.
