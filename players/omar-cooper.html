Ball Keep Take

Omar Cooper Jr. is a turn-of-the-first flyer on a Jets team that needs flyers to hit. Indiana's 2025 WR reel is speed and playmaking. Our board has him 11th. That is 'I still need a receiver and I believe in the athlete' money, not 'I believe in the Jets' money.

Wilson is the alpha. Cooper is a dart. If the dart hits, you look smart on a podcast. If it doesn't, you took a WR at 1.11 instead of Price or Simpson. Superflex can absorb that. 1QB rebuilds can absorb that. Contenders cannot.

Take him if Tyson/Tate/Lemon/Concepcion are gone and you refuse to take a tight end. That is a real strategy. It is not a safe one.
