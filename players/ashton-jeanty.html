The tape

Ashton Jeanty was the 2025 draft's running-back hammer, and The Keep has not cooled on him. Seven sources, average 13.57, Keep #13. The 188-yard game against Houston is the 'he's a feature back' tape, not a combine-profile hope. Las Vegas using him as a week-to-week answer is the dynasty dream: young, expensive, and actually getting the touches.

Superflex still ranks several quarterbacks and a couple of receivers ahead of him, which is correct process. Among backs only Bijan, Gibbs, Achane, and the incoming Love are in his immediate neighborhood on our long boards. That is elite company for a player who just finished year one.

Plus

  • Keep #13 with seven boards - the 2025 rookie back who cleared the 'is he real?' bar.
  • Youth, draft capital, and Raiders volume keep him in the Gibbs/Bijan conversation for 2027-29.

Minus

  • Year-two running backs can still hit the second-season committee or efficiency dip.
  • Redraft has him in the 20s, not as a 1.01; 2026 weekly RB1 is not fully locked.
