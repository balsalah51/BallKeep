The tape

Garrett Wilson is the 'how is he only Keep 37?' receiver until you watch a Jets possession that dies at second-and-14. Three boards, average 37. The 107-yard Week 6 clip is the talent: stem, break, YAC. The ranking is the franchise.

Superflex managers who already have two WR1s should be happy to hold Wilson as a WR2 with leftover upside if the Jets passing game ever becomes average. Managers who need a weekly WR1 should have taken JSN. That sentence is mean and true.

Plus

  • Keep #37 - Jets WR1 talent that has survived multiple quarterbacks.
  • Yates still has him as a WR2 you can start; the route running never dipped.
  • Hot #3: FantasyLife (Sep 8): 26, three 1,000-yard seasons already. Buy before the Week 1 reminder. Clean swap for Tate, or even a Nabers downgrade. - FantasyLife

Minus

  • New York's offense can be a weekly void; talent has not always equaled WR1 finishes.
  • Jets can always add another mouth (Sadiq, Cooper on our rookie board).
