The tape

Drake Maye is still a top Superflex name, just not the 1.01 argument some short lists made him. FantasyPros ECR, Erickson, and Fitzmaurice still rank him first. Keep does not. After the Seattle opener he sits four spots lower on every Ball Keep list. The long boards (PFN, Dynasty Nerds) already parked him behind Allen.

The 2025 film is why he was priced like a finished product. The AFC run clip is still real: a long-striding quarterback who can push the ball and scramble. New England's supporting cast is not a weekly apology. Week 1 was the other tape. Three late picks, and he called the mistakes unacceptable after the game.

Minus
