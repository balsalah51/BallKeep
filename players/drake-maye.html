The tape

Drake Maye is the dynasty argument of 2026. Three of our short expert lists (FantasyPros ECR, Andrew Erickson, Pat Fitzmaurice) rank him the Superflex 1.01, Derek Brown has him second, and the long boards (PFN, Dynasty Nerds) park him third behind only Allen. The Keep average is 2.43. That is a 23-year-old passer being priced like a finished product.

The 2025 film is why. The Can't-Miss clip we embedded is the AFC run in miniature: Maye dropping a touchdown and a receiver finishing like a superhero. New England's offense finally looks like a place where a long-striding quarterback can push the ball and scramble without the supporting cast being a weekly apology. Superflex managers who waited on the 2024 draft capital got paid.

Plus
