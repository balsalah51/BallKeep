Ball Keep take

Ladd McConkey is the receiver who wins on alignment and feel. Keep #38, three-board average 38.67. The 58-yard pickup we clipped from 2025 is not a trick play; it is McConkey getting open and Herbert trusting the timing. That pairing is why dynasty still has him inside the top 40 Superflex names.

He will never look like Chase. He does not have to. Slot receivers who run like McConkey and play with Herbert become PPR monsters in the right year. Our Keep rank is a 'we believe, we are not in love' rank.

If your league is still calling him a gadget, buy. If your league is calling him JSN, sell. The truth is a top-40 dynasty WR with a real quarterback, which is more than most WR3s on waivers will ever have.
