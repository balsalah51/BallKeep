Ball Keep Take

Fernando Mendoza is the 2026 Superflex rookie quarterback. Indiana's 2025 Heisman reel is a big passer with velocity and enough accuracy to make Las Vegas spend the first overall pick. Our rookie board has him second behind only Love, and the note is honest: clear 1.02 in Superflex, sit-behind-Cousins year-one risk.

That sit is the entire minus. Superflex managers drafting Mendoza 1.02 are paying for 2027-2031, not for 16 starts in 2026. If your league mates are terrified of a sit, you may get him at 1.03/1.04 and should smash. If they are terrified of Love because 'running backs,' you take Love and let them have the sit.

The Raiders pairing a first-overall quarterback with Jeanty is a long-term stack we like on paper. On the field, 2026 may be a redshirt in everything but name. Rank Mendoza as a Superflex first-round rookie, not as a redraft QB1.
