Ball Keep Take

Christian Watson is a Hot name because the second-half numbers were already WR2 and the room just opened. Sports Arena and Draft Sharks: WR21 in FPPG Weeks 8-18 on a 68% route share, Doubs and Wicks gone. That is not a projection. That is math plus a depth chart. The 2025-26 Packers reel is Watson running through the paint on go balls Love can actually throw.

The minus is the injury history that created the price. If you need a guaranteed 17 games, take St. Brown. If you need a WR2 with WR1 upside at a discount, take Watson. The Lemon Cold trade (Lemon for Watson) is this page's cousin: win-now teams want Watson, rebuilds want Lemon. We think the win-now teams are right for 2026.

Buy him from managers who only remember the IL stints. Start him if Love is right. Do not pay Chase money. Pay WR2 money for WR2 production that might be WR1 if the route share holds.
