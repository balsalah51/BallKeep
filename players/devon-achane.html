Ball Keep Take

De'Von Achane is the efficiency argument in cleats. Keep #20, Yates PPR eighth, and our standard board actually bumps him because the touchdowns are real, not just PPR padding. The 121-yard Week 18 clip is the player: a crease, a missed tackle, a defense chasing a blur.

Miami will always threaten to 'committee' him, and he will always make 14 touches look like 22. That is the dynasty hold. It is also why some Superflex managers refuse to take him in the first two rounds — they have been burned by small backs who missed four games. Achane's 2025 health was good enough that we are not putting him in the injury-poster tier.

If you play PPR, he is a top-10 pick and we will not argue. If you play Superflex dynasty, he is a Keep 20 who you take after the quarterbacks and the true 1.01 skill names. If you play standard and think speed doesn't count without 25 carries, watch the clip and try again.
