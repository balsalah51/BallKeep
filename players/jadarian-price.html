Ball Keep Take

Jadarian Price is the rookie running back who climbed our board after the draft. Sixth in the class, with a note about projected Seattle volume. The Notre Dame 2025 versatility reel — the Seahawks' own 'newest Seahawk' pack — is a back who can catch and run, which is how you steal snaps in a modern offense.

He is not Jeremiyah Love. He does not need to be. If Seattle's backfield is a door, Price is a player who can walk through it. Superflex rookie drafts should have him in the late first/early second. Redraft should only care if the Week 1 snap share is loud.

The minus is every Seahawks backfield and the fact that Love took the 1.01 shine from the same school. Price can still eat. Pay late-first money, not Love money.
