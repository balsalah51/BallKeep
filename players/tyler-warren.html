The tape

Tyler Warren is the 2025 tight end The Keep put in the early 40s because the position is thin and the Colts actually used him. Average 42, KTC hotter at 33. That KTC number is the market saying 'we will pay for a young TE who isn't a blocker-only.' The 2025 Colts reel is Warren as a receiver: choice routes, seams, enough athleticism to threaten linebackers.

Next to Taylor, Warren's job is to be the passing-game answer when defenses load the box. That is a good job. It is not Bowers' job. Rank him as TE3 on a Superflex board, TE2 in premium, and a hold in every league that missed the Bowers/McBride/Loveland cluster.

  • Youth at TE; three-board support rather than a one-source dart.

Minus
