Ball Keep Take

Jaylen Waddle in Denver is a sentence Ball Keep is still evaluating in public. Yates 44th, Keep off the top 50. The 2025 midseason Broncos All-22 clip is speed in a new offense: jets, crossing routes, Nix taking shots. That is more interesting than another year as Miami's WR2.

Dynasty wanted Waddle to be a WR1 three years ago. He is a WR3 with WR2 upside now, which is still useful and no longer a 1.01. If the Broncos usage spikes, The Keep will have him in the 50s next cycle and we will all pretend we knew. If it doesn't, he is a speed bench piece.

Buy only if the other manager still has 2021 Waddle in their notes app. Sell if someone pays London money. The likely outcome is a WR3 you can start in a pinch.
