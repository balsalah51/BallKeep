Ball Keep Take

Lamar Jackson is the quarterback every Superflex league still regrets fading. Our seven-board average puts him 10th. PFN has him fifth. The 2025 clip of Andrews racing down the sideline is the play-action shot Lamar has always had, now with a contract-year receiver group and a Ravens offense that can still tilt into him as a runner.

The expert split is the story. Fitzmaurice and Erickson still have him inside the top six. Brown has him 27th. That is not a talent dispute. It is a 'how do I age a scrambling MVP?' dispute. Ball Keep splits the difference at 10, which keeps him in the first round of Superflex startups and out of the Maye/Allen/Daniels pantheon.

Redraft loves the weekly ceiling (Yates in the mid-30s overall is still a QB1 pick). Dynasty has to ask how many more years the rushing volume holds. If you need a two-year window, Lamar is a smash. If you need a five-year QB1, the kids are cheaper in years and not cheaper on our board — which is exactly why Lamar still has a market.
