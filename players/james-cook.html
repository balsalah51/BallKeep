The tape

James Cook is the split between redraft and dynasty in one name. Yates has him as a top-10 PPR pick. Our standard board bumps him into the top 10 because the touchdowns were not PPR noise. The Keep has him 27th on two long boards because Superflex managers are already asking who the Bills' back will be in 2028.

The 2025 three-touchdown game against New England is the advertisement: vision, enough speed, a quarterback who sells play action. Cook is not a third-down toy. He is a scoring back on the best offense in the AFC when Allen is rolling.

Plus

  • Yates PPR top 10 and our standard board even higher - 2025 Bills RB1 weeks were real.
  • Receiving work plus Allen's offense keeps a weekly floor most 'dumb running backs' lack.

Minus

  • Keep #27 on only two long boards - dynasty is already looking at the 2027-28 age tax.
  • Buffalo will always threaten to add another back; feature-back security is never full.
