Jordan Mason is a Hot board process pick. Draft Sharks: cheap potential starter in a new Minnesota scheme, buy before a spike week. That is not poetry. That is waiver-wire discipline. The 2025 top-plays breakdown is a physical runner who can take 18 carries without the offense dying.
