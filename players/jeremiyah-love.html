The tape

Jeremiyah Love is the 2026 draft's skill-position hammer. Notre Dame's 2025 tape — 1,372 yards and 18 rushing scores in only 12 games, after a 2024 that already had him in a national title game — is why Arizona spent the third overall pick on a running back for the first time in a generation. Dynasty Dealer, FantasyPros, and PFF all locked him as the rookie 1.01. Ball Keep's Superflex board already has him 21st overall among all players, not just rookies.

The college clip and the Cardinals 'shows out' reel are the same player: vision, burst, and enough receiving to be a down-to-down back, not a committee toy. He combined for 40 touchdowns over two seasons and still has a tiny career workload. That is the dynasty dream: production without the mileage.

Plus

  • Locked 1.01 on our 2026 rookie board (Dealer/FP/PFF). Keep #21 as a drafted Cardinal.
  • Notre Dame 2025: 1,372 yards, 18 rushing TDs in 12 games, 6.9 yards per carry, still not 21.
  • Locked 1.01 · elite RB1 capital

Minus

  • Arizona already has Conner/Benson/Allgeier noise even if Love is the lead of the future.
  • Running backs taken third overall still have to hit immediately or the industry calls it a reach.
