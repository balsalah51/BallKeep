The tape

Omarion Hampton's 2025 breakout-game reel is the reason he is Keep #24 instead of a 'wait and see' rookie hold. Three long boards, average 24.33. The Chargers used him like a lead back, and the film looks like a lead back: inside runs, enough speed to finish, a complementary piece next to Herbert and McConkey rather than a gadget.

Dynasty wants young running backs attached to good quarterbacks. This is that profile. Jeanty went higher because of draft capital and the Raiders' usage. Hampton is the next name in that sentence. If you missed Jeanty in 2025 startups, Hampton is the 2026 version of 'I still need a back who will be here in 2028.'

Plus

  • Keep #24 — 2025 Chargers feature-back tape, not a committee hope.
  • Youth plus Herbert is a cleaner long-term RB bet than most 27-year-old win-now names.

Minus

  • Year-two backfield math can still get messy in Los Angeles.
  • Redraft 20s is respect, not a 1.01; 2026 RB1 is earned, not granted.
