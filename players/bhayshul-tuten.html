Minus

Ball Keep Take

Bhayshul Tuten is the last name on our standard redraft sheet and nowhere on The Keep top 50, which is an honest way to say 'interesting young rusher, not a building block yet.' The 2025 Jaguars top-plays reel is a back who can finish and hit the edge. Standard scoring noticed. PPR was less sure.

If Jacksonville's backfield opens, Tuten is a name we will write 800 more words about next cycle. If it doesn't, he is a stash. Superflex rebuilders can hold a taxi-squad piece. Contenders should only roster him as a dart.

Do not take him over Judkins or Irving in dynasty. Do take him in the last round of a standard draft if you like the tape. That is the 50th-ranked standard running back special.
