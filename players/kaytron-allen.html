Ball Keep Take

Kaytron Allen is the last name on our 2026 rookie sheet: Day 3 dart, Penn State bruiser, Commanders preseason 23-carry debut. That 23-carry clip is the plus — someone in Washington wanted to see him as a hammer. The minus is that preseason hammers become September inactives every year.

If the Commanders' backfield opens, Allen is a waiver-wire hero in redraft and a nice taxi hit in dynasty. If it doesn't, he was Rookie 20 and you spent a third. Superflex thirds are for this.

Do not draft him before Singleton unless you are in love. Do draft him at the end of a rookie draft if you like mean runners. We like mean runners. We like them cheaper.
