The tape

Malik Nabers is the 'pay for the film, live with the franchise' receiver. Keep #17, three-board average of 15 even with KTC at 15. The 2025 Giants highlights are Nabers winning anyway: contested catches, yards after contact, a target share that looks like a WR1 on a team that is not always a WR1 environment.

Jaxson Dart's development is the 2026 swing. If the passing game takes a step, Nabers is a top-12 dynasty receiver with room to run. If it doesn't, he is still the best player in that huddle and a weekly WR2 with WR1 spikes. Superflex can live with that. Redraft feels every dart (sorry) that sails high.

Plus

  • Keep #17, KTC 15th - the Giants' alpha with WR1 upside still in front of him.
  • Youth plus target dominance is the dynasty profile teams overpay for in startups.

Minus

  • Yates PPR 25th: 2026 weekly floor still tied to New York's quarterback development.
  • Giants offense can be a volume desert even when Nabers is clearly the best player on the field.
