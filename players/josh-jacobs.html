The tape

Josh Jacobs is how you win a 2026 redraft without winning a 2029 dynasty. Yates 20th in PPR, higher in standard, absent from The Keep top 50. The Packers touchdown run we clipped is Jacobs' job description: 70-yard drive, you finish it, we all go home.

Love's passing game (Watson, Kraft) can steal a few red-zone looks. Jacobs still gets enough to be an RB2 you start without a puzzle. Superflex managers rebuilding should sell him to a contender for a 2027 pick that actually matters.
