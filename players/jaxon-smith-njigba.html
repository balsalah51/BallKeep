Ball Keep Take

Jaxon Smith-Njigba is the receiver the industry decided was real in 2025. Ball Keep has him fifth in Superflex, ahead of Gibbs on average, with Brown and Fitzmaurice both inside the top 10. The old 'he's a slot specialist' fade is dead. The 100-yard clip against Pittsburgh is the weekly version: get him the ball in space and let him finish.

Dynasty likes him even more than redraft standard does, which is the tell. PPR captures the volume. Superflex still has to wait until the quarterbacks are gone, but among skill players JSN is in the Chase/Puka/Bijan band. Seattle's offense has become a place where a receiver can put up WR1 weeks without waiting for a once-a-year explosion.

The risk is paying WR1 money for a player whose 2025 might have been the leap rather than the floor. We think it is the floor. If you are a manager still ranking him as a WR2 because of the 2023-24 tape, The Keep is the other side of that trade.
