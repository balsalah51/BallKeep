Ball Keep take

DJ Moore is on the Cold board because the name is ahead of the production. The Fantasy Footballers: 29 years old, new team in Buffalo, 60-790 last year, sell the name. That is a dagger and also a trade instruction. The 2025-26 highlight mix is still a good route runner. Good route runners on new teams with 60-catch years are WR3s, not WR1s.

If a Bills stacker will pay last year's Moore price, sell. If the market has already moved him to WR4, you can hold and hope Buffalo's passing game is a rising tide. Superflex rebuilders should not have Moore. Contenders can start him as a flex if the looks are there.

We are not putting him on The Keep top 50. We are putting him on Cold so you stop paying for 2023. Thank you in advance.
