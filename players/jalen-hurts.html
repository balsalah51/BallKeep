Ball Keep Take

Jalen Hurts is the Superflex player who breaks modelers and wins championships. Derek Brown still has him third. Dynasty Nerds and KeepTradeCut have him 26th. Ball Keep averages that into 19, which is our way of saying the rushing touchdowns are real and the passing-game ceiling is no longer a top-five SF argument.

The 2025 three-touchdown clip is the weekly Hurts experience: sneak, keeper, enough throwing to keep defenses honest. Philadelphia will keep scoring. Hurts will keep vulturing. That is a QB1 floor in Superflex even when the box score looks like a 'game manager plus legs' night.

The minus is the industry split itself. If your league is full of Brown-stans, you cannot buy Hurts. If your league is full of KTC-stans, you can. We would rather have Maye or Daniels for the next five years and Hurts for the next two. That sentence is the ranking.
