The tape

Jonathon Brooks is Cold because two ACLs in 13 months is not a puzzle, it is a medical chart. FantasyPros: still unproven, market near RB26. That RB26 price is what people are trying to sell you as a bargain. It is only a bargain if you believe in a third health chapter. The 2025 return clip is a player trying to come back. Trying is not producing.

Superflex rebuilders can stash him on a taxi if the cost is a late third. Contenders should not roster a hope. Redraft should not draft a hope. The market near RB26 is the trap — it feels cheap relative to 2024 ADP and expensive relative to two ACLs.
