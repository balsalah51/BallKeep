Ball Keep Take

Breece Hall is the first name on this site where The Keep and the Cold board are having the same conversation out loud. Keep #40 on two long boards. Yates still has him around 31 in PPR. Sports Arena's sell note is blunt: if a manager will still pay true top-10 running back value, you take it, because the efficiency and target share have slipped.

The 2025 Week 1 146-yard clip is the reminder of what you are selling: burst, long speed, a back who can end a game in four touches. The ranking is the rest of the season and the Jets' offensive fog. Talent is not the debate. Trajectory is.

If the offer is a late first, you hold. If the offer is a young WR1 or a 2027 first plus a usable piece, you listen. Cold does not mean 'drop him.' It means 'stop pretending he is still Gibbs.'
