The tape

Justin Herbert is the Superflex QB you draft when you want to stop thinking. Seven sources, average 16.43, Keep #18. Erickson loves him (fifth). Brown is colder (26th). The long boards park him in the teens. That average is a franchise quarterback with a real supporting cast for the first time in a while: McConkey, Hampton, and a Chargers offense that can be boring in a good way.

The 2025 Best Plays vs. Kansas City clip is Herbert's plus: velocity, sideline throws, and enough mobility to keep a play alive without being Lamar. He will never be the rushing-QB cheat code. He does not have to be. Superflex leagues that already have one dual-threat still need a second passer who can throw for 280 without a meltdown.

Plus

  • Keep #18 on seven boards - the cleanest 'he's just a really good QB' ranking on the sheet.
  • Chargers weapons (McConkey, Hampton) give 2026 a higher floor than the 'Herbert is sad' years.

Minus

  • Brown 26 / Erickson 5 is a wild expert split for a veteran.
  • No Yates top-50 slot: 1QB redraft can wait, Superflex cannot if you missed Maye/Allen.
