The tape

Trevor Lawrence is on our Hot board for a reason the ranking list alone does not capture. FantasyPros' Trade Value Chart show and the Fantasy Footballers both treated the second half of 2025 - 26 fantasy points per game - as the version that closes the gap on the Mahomes/QB8 band. Keep #28 is the long-board echo of that, not a sudden 1.01.

The 2025-26 highlight mix is Lawrence when the timing is right: deep overs, designed rollouts, Jacksonville looking like a passing game instead of a hope. Superflex managers who faded him into QB2 range last summer are the ones now asking for a first to move him. That is how Hot names work.

Plus

  • Hot board: FantasyPros TVC + YouTube say he closed the gap on Mahomes in the QB8-9 band after a 26 FPPG second half.
  • Keep #28 - still a starting Superflex QB1 with weapons (Brian Thomas, Tuten) if the second half is the real version.

Minus

  • Two-board Keep mix is thin; this is a 'buy the second half' ranking, not a seven-source lock.
  • Jaguars supporting-cast crowding (Meyers/Washington) is a Cold note for his WR1, which cuts both ways.
