The tape

Eli Stowers is the second tight end in a class that only has so many. Vanderbilt's 2025 highlights are a pass-catching tight end, and Philadelphia spending capital there is interesting even in a crowded room. Our board has him 10th: late first / early second TE.

The Eagles will not make Stowers a 2026 centerpiece. They might make him a 2027 chess piece. Superflex TE-premium managers can take him in the second. Everyone else can take a WR. Lemon's Cold note is your warning about this room: packed is packed.

Plus

Minus
