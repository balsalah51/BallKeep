The tape

Kyler Murray is the hottest name on the Hot board because of price, not because we discovered him. DLF's August 16 note is the entire thesis: when healthy he was a locked top-10 Superflex quarterback, and the market currently prices him like a mid-first rookie pick. That gap is the buy. The 2025 three-TD game against the Rams is the player: rushing, arm talent, chaos in the best way.

Minnesota is a new chapter. New chapters can fix a player or expose one. The Hot tag is a bet that the price already assumes the worst and that a healthy Kyler is still a SF QB1. If you cannot stand the durability, do not buy him. If you can, this is the cheapest top-10 QB talent on the site.
