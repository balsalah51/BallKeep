Ball Keep Take

Caleb Williams is the Superflex hold you cannot trade for a 'win-now' QB2 and feel smart about. Keep #12, KTC 10th, Dynasty Nerds eighth. The Bears have stacked Odunze, Loveland, and Burden around him, which is how you build a five-year passing game even when the weekly box score still looks like a work in progress.

The Can't-Miss scramble from 2025 is both the plus and the minus. Plus: he can still win a down when everything dies. Minus: Superflex managers are tired of paying elite capital for 'when everything dies.' The 2026 bet is that the structure around him finally lets the timing throws show up on early downs, not just on broken plays.

He is absent from Yates' top 50 because 1QB redraft can wait on Chicago. Superflex cannot. If your league starts two quarterbacks, Williams is a top-15 Keep name. If it starts one, he is a late QB1/high QB2 you draft with your gut, not with our keystone board.
