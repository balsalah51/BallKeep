The tape

Drake London is the receiver who keeps threatening to join the first tier and keeps getting ranked in the second. Keep #23, Yates 17th in PPR. The 2025 highlights are London winning 50-50s and working the sideline. He is not a gadget. He is a true X who happens to share an offense with Bijan.

That pairing is the plus and the minus. Plus: Atlanta has two Keep top-25 players and defenses cannot ignore either. Minus: Bijan's rushing volume can steal possessions, and London's ceiling weeks need negative game script or a quarterback hitting him in rhythm. Superflex managers who already have a WR1 should be thrilled to add London as WR2. Managers who need a 1.01 receiver should look higher on The Keep.

Plus

  • Keep #23, Yates PPR WR2 with WR1 weeks next to Bijan.
  • Age and target share still point up; Atlanta has to throw when it trails.

Minus

  • Three-board average of 23 is a 'very good, not alpha' cluster with Pickens/Egbuka.
  • Quarterback play in Atlanta is the weekly variance that keeps him out of the JSN tier.
