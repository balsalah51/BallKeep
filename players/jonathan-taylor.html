Ball Keep Take

Jonathan Taylor is the reddest redraft name on a dynasty site. Yates has him seventh in PPR. Our standard board has him fourth because that is what 20-carry touchdown backs do in non-PPR. The Colts 2025 reel is Taylor as a hammer: inside, outside, enough burst left to finish.

The Keep at 31 is not a smear. It is Superflex math. Quarterbacks and young skill players eat the first two rounds, and a running back with Taylor's injury history does not get the Gibbs/Bijan premium. You can win a 2026 redraft title with Taylor as your RB1. You cannot build a 2029 roster around him as your only expensive back.

If a dynasty manager still has Taylor ranked like 2021, buy the rest of their team. If a redraft manager is letting him slide because of 'the injury,' take him and stop overthinking. Format is the ranking.
