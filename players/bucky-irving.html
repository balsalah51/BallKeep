The tape

Bucky Irving is the young Buccaneer back who keeps showing up on film as a pass catcher. Yates 49th, standard 44th. The fade-route touchdown Can't-Miss is the plus: this is not a two-down plodder. PPR leagues should care. Superflex rebuilders should care a little.

Tampa Bay will keep a committee. That is already priced into the 40s. What would move Irving onto The Keep is a month of 18-touch games, not another cute receiving TD. Until then he is a hold in dynasty and a PPR flex in redraft.

Plus

  • Yates PPR 49th / STD 44th - 2025 Buccaneers receiving-back juice (fade-route TD clip).
  • Youth; this is a name that can still climb onto The Keep if Tampa commits.

Minus

  • Committee in Tampa is the base case, not the nightmare case.
  • Off The Keep top 50 - prove the rushing volume before we pay RB2 dynasty money.
