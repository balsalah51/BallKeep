The tape

Antonio Williams is a mid-second receiver into a Commanders passing game that already has Daniels and McLaurin. Clemson's 2025 reel is a WR with enough juice to contribute. Our board has him 14th. That is 'I like the offense' money.

The plus is Jayden Daniels creating explosives and a room that can use a third/fourth receiver who can actually play. The minus is that those explosives will go to McLaurin first (see the Can't-Miss on Daniels' page). Williams is a stash with a good address.

Plus

  • Rookie #14 - mid second, Clemson 2025 WR reel, Commanders dart next to Daniels/McLaurin.
  • Good offense; a piece of Washington's passing game is a real piece.

Minus

  • McLaurin (and the rest of the room) will eat first; year-one WR4 is the base.
  • Mid-second WRs on good teams can still be inactive on Sundays.
