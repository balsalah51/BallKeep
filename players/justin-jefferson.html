Ball Keep take

Justin Jefferson is the first 'is the throne gone?' receiver on The Keep. We still have him ninth. Yates still has him inside the top 12 PPR. The 2025-26 highlight reel is still Jefferson: stems, stack releases, and the catch radius that made him a 1.01 for half a decade. He did not become a WR2 overnight.

What changed is the company. Chase, JSN, Nacua, and St. Brown have eaten the 'untouchable WR1' premium, and our short expert lists (Brown/Erickson/Fitzmaurice) did not even publish a Jefferson rank in the slices we captured. That leaves him on PFN, Dynasty Nerds, and KTC — still elite, no longer unanimous.

Minnesota's quarterback situation is the minus you actually price. Jefferson's talent does not need a superstar passer to be a WR1, but it does need competent volume. Dynasty still holds him as a top-10 Superflex skill player. Redraft still drafts him as a low-end WR1. We are not selling for a WR3 package. We are not paying 2022 prices either.
