Jonah Coleman is an early-second running back into a Broncos committee that already put RJ Harvey on our Cold board because of J.K. Dobbins. Washington's 2025 clip against Michigan is a physical runner. Denver can use a physical runner. They can also use three of them and make all of you sad.
