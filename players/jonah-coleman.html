The tape

Jonah Coleman is an early-second running back into a Broncos committee that already put RJ Harvey on our Cold board because of J.K. Dobbins. Washington's 2025 clip against Michigan is a physical runner. Denver can use a physical runner. They can also use three of them and make all of you sad.

The rank is 12th in the rookie class because someone will be the Broncos' back, and Coleman has a punch. The minus is that it might not be him. Superflex taxi squads are for this profile. Starting lineups are not, until the snap share says so.

Plus

  • Broncos offense can score; a piece of it is still a piece.
