Ball Keep Take

Cam Skattebo is the young Giant back redraft has already put in the 30s. Yates 38th, standard 34th. The 2025 debut/preseason physical reel is a player who runs angry, which is a scouting-report cliché and also, in Skattebo's case, accurate. Giants football needs someone who runs angry.

The Keep has not put him in the top 50 because Superflex still has 50 names with cleaner situations. That can change fast if the snap share prints. Young backs on bad teams are how Keep boards look stupid a year later — in both directions.

Take him in redraft as an RB3 with upside. In dynasty, he's a hold if you have him and a 'don't overpay' if you don't. The Giants will tell us who he is by November. Until then, the ranking is a placeholder with a stiff-arm.
