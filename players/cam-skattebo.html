The tape

Cam Skattebo is the young Giant back redraft has already put in the 30s. Yates 38th, standard 34th. The 2025 debut/preseason physical reel is a player who runs angry, which is a scouting-report cliché and also, in Skattebo's case, accurate. Giants football needs someone who runs angry.
