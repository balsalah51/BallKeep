The tape

Luther Burden III is the rare name that is both Keep #48 and Cold. Those two things are not a contradiction. The Keep says the talent and the 2025 138-yard game against San Francisco are real. The Cold board says the market asking WR19 is not. Sports Arena's trade: Burden for A.J. Brown if that is the ask. We agree with the trade and still like Burden as a long-term hold if you already have him at a reasonable cost.

Chicago will throw to a lot of people. That caps the 2026 weekly ceiling and does not kill the 2028 ceiling. Rookie receivers on crowded teams are where impatient managers donate first-round value.
