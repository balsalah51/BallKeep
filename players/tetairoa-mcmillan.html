Ball Keep Take

Tetairoa McMillan is the 2025 draft receiver The Keep is still willing to treat as a top-30 Superflex asset. Average 27, Keep #26. The early-season Panthers reel is the profile: catch radius, go-ball threats, a team that drafted him to be the offense's answer downfield.

Carolina will keep being a noisy franchise, and that is already in the redraft rank (Yates 42nd). Dynasty does not have to care about one slow September. It has to care whether McMillan is a WR1 in 2027. Our long boards say yes, with a lower floor than JSN or Nacua because the quarterback situation is not Seattle or Los Angeles.

If a win-now manager wants to send you a 28-year-old WR2 plus a second for McMillan, that is a smash accept on a rebuild. If a rebuild manager wants McMillan plus a first for Chase, hang up. He is a Keep 26, not a Keep 5.
