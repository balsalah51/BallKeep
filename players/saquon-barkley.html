Ball Keep take

Saquon Barkley's 60-yard Can't-Miss on the first touch of a game is the talent that never left. Yates still has him as a top-22 PPR pick. Our standard board bumps him. The Keep does not put him in the top 50 because Superflex dynasty is about 2028, and Barkley's odometer is not a secret.

Eagles game script plus Hurts sneaks is the minus on scoring. Saquon's long speed is the plus on everything else. Redraft should still take him as an RB2 with RB1 weeks. Dynasty should treat him like CMC's slightly younger cousin: useful, expensive in redraft, not a rebuild piece.

If a contender offers you a first for Barkley in Superflex, you smash accept unless you are also a contender. If you are also a contender, you start him and stop reading player pages.
