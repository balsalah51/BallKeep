The tape

Bijan Robinson is the running back The Keep is willing to take in the same breath as Chase. Dynasty Nerds have him second overall. Yates has him as a top-two PPR pick. Standard scoring bumps him even further. The 238-yard game we clipped from 2025 is not a meme week; it is the ceiling that makes managers ignore every 'running backs don't last' sermon for one more startup.

What the experts are buying is the complete profile: between-the-tackles power, speed to the edge, and enough routes to keep him on the field on third down. At 24 he is still on the young side of a feature back's prime. That is rare in a 2026 running-back market full of 27-year-old win-now names.

Plus

  • Keep #4 and redraft RB1/RB2 territory in both PPR and standard.
  • Age-24 workhorse with the receiving chops to survive a negative game script.

Minus

  • Atlanta's supporting cast and game script can cap weekly explosiveness even when the talent is 1.01.
  • Seven-board average of 5.71 is a hair behind Chase — some SF managers will always take the WR first.
