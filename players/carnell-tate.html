The tape

Carnell Tate is the receiver our rookie board calls the safest, and the Titans spent the fourth overall pick to put him next to Cam Ward. Ohio State's 2025 tape is a WR who wins with strength at the catch point, finds the holes in zone, and does not need a gadget package to produce. Dynasty Dealer, FantasyPros, and PFF all had him third in the class behind Love and Mendoza.

The Keep already ranking him 43rd among all Superflex players is the 'we think he's a real NFL starter' stamp. That is aggressive for a player who has not played an NFL down. It is also how you treat a top-five pick at receiver in a class that is not loaded with them.

Plus

  • Rookie #3, Titans' first-round WR for Cam Ward - safest WR in the 2026 class on our sheet.
  • Ohio State 2025 weapon tape; Keep #43 already among all players, not just rookies.
  • Safest WR · Day-1 starter with Ward

Minus

  • Tennessee passing volume is the career-long question even with Ward.
  • First-year WRs attached to young QBs can take a year to hit weekly WR2.
  • Cold #6: FantasyPros (Aug 9): dynasty ECR WR14. Titans OL ranked 31st and he was never a 900-yard college WR. Sell the rookie-class premium. - FantasyPros
