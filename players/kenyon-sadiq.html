The tape

Kenyon Sadiq is the tight end of the 2026 class on our board. Oregon's 2025 reel — the Jets' own 'was a problem' pack — is a move tight end who can threaten the seam. New York adding him to Garrett Wilson is a passing-game bet that the Jets desperately need to make.

Late first in Superflex, earlier in TE premium. That is the rank. Wilson is the alpha. Sadiq is the hope for 2027. If you need a 2026 tight end, you should have Bowers. If you need a 2028 tight end, Sadiq is the pick after the WRs you actually like.

Plus

  • Tight end scarcity makes the class TE1 a first-round pick in premium and a late first in Superflex.

Minus
