The tape

Germie Bernard's Alabama 2025 reel is a receiver with enough twitch to interest the Steelers in the middle of the draft. Our board has him 16th. That is a dart into a franchise that will run first and throw second, which is a hard place to need fantasy points and a fine place to stash an athlete.

If Pittsburgh's veterans fade, Bernard can become a thing. If they don't, you took a WR in the mid-second and lived. Superflex benches are long. Use them for this.

Plus

  • Rookie #16 - mid second, Alabama 2025 highlights, Steelers WR dart.
  • Pittsburgh can unexpectedly feed a rookie WR when the veterans fade.
  • Hot #1: Six for 80 on nine targets, 94 percent of the routes, Pittman on IR, Wilson out a couple of weeks. Boone put him first on the Week 6 waiver mash. Tampa Bay is Sunday. Pay the chair before the room remembers the name. - Yahoo Sports, FantasyPros

Minus

  • Steelers passing volume is a lifestyle minus.
  • Mid-second WR with no clear WR1 path is a camp battle, not a rank you die on.
