The tape

Brian Thomas Jr. is Cold #1, which is a price warning, not a talent funeral. DLF and Sports Arena: Jakobi Meyers and Parker Washington crowding targets, while ADP still treats BTJ like a high WR2. The 2025 one-handed Can't-Miss from Lawrence is the talent. The ranking is the room.

If you can sell him for true WR2-plus value, the Cold board says take it. If the offer is a WR3 and a late second, you hold, because the player on the clip is not a WR3. Superflex managers should be honest about 2026 weekly floor: it might be WR3 with WR1 spikes, not locked WR2.
