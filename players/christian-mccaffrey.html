The tape

Christian McCaffrey is the player who makes dynasty and redraft look like different sports. Yates has him as a top-four PPR pick. Our standard board has him third. The Keep does not have him in the top 50. Sports Arena's Hot note is only for contenders: PFM RB9 versus ECR RB15, buy if you are winning now, not if you are rebuilding.

The 2025 Can't-Miss touchdown - outrunning everyone - is still the best running-back film in football when he's right. That is why redraft cannot fade him. It is also why Superflex rebuilders cannot afford him. You are buying 2026. You are not buying 2029.
