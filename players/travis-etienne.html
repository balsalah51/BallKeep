The tape

Travis Etienne Jr. is a change-of-scenery back with a PPR skill set. Yates 37th, standard 33rd, Keep off the top 50. The two-TD clip is the plus: speed to the edge, enough receiving to stay on the field. Saints football can use that.

Dynasty already paid for Etienne once. It will not pay again unless the Saints usage is undeniable for two months. If you are contending in Superflex, he is a fine RB3. If you are rebuilding, he is a name you send to the Olave owner for a third and a prayer - wait, get more than a third. Get a second if they are desperate.

Plus

  • Yates PPR 37th / STD 33rd - new Saints chapter with enough receiving to be a PPR RB2/3.
  • Explosiveness on the 2025 two-TD reel is still there when the crease is there.

Minus

  • Off The Keep top 50 - the Jaguars years already spent the dynasty premium.
  • New Orleans backfield math can become a committee without your permission.
