Ball Keep Take

Brock Purdy at Keep #39 is a Superflex starter ranking, not a slight. Three boards around 40. The 2025 sideline throw to Deebo (in the Can't-Miss clip) is Purdy's plus: anticipation, placement, a 49ers offense that still knows how to manufacture explosives when the pieces are there.

The minus is the pieces. Pearsall is on our Cold board with a long absence. CMC is a win-now Hot name, not a five-year stack. Purdy's dynasty value is tied to San Francisco remaining a functioning passing game, not to Purdy suddenly becoming Maye.

Hold him as QB2 in Superflex. Do not trade Maye for him because 'Purdy wins games.' Winning games is for real football. Superflex is for weekly floors and five-year windows. Purdy is a good QB2. Rank him like one.
