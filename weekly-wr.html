How Week 1 WR is built.

How is the weekly board built?

Super Aggregate of every Week N board that ranked the name: 50% FantasyPros ECR, 50% RotoWire projections, FantasyPros projections, and 4for4. Unranked on a board is a skip, never a last-place dump.

What is the Proj column?

RotoWire PPR points for this week, via Sleeper. Blank means that model skipped the name.

When does this refresh?

A Monday and Tuesday job re-scrapes the public boards and rebuilds the pages.
