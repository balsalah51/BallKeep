Davis and Young aren’t enough. Wizards fall to Charlotte Hornets

Aggregate

The Wizards couldn’t keep Moussa Diabate from getting offensive rebounds or Brandon Miller from scoring from everywhere, so they lost to the Hornets in Charlotte despite solid games from Anthony Davis and Trae Young. Diabate grabbed eight offensive boards in the first half and finished with 11 for the game - a new career high. Board reading of SB Nation.
