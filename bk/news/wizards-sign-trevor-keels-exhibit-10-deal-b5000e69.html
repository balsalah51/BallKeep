Report: Wizards sign Trevor Keels to Exhibit-10 deal

Aggregate

P.J. Washington: The Washington Wizards have signed Trevor Keels to an Exhibit-10 contract, per Spotrac’s Keith Smith. Keels, 23, will spend training camp with the Wizards and likely join the Capital City Go-Go following the preseason. It’s a homecoming for the Duke product, who is from Clinton, Maryland, and played high school basketball at Paul VI Catholic. Board reading of SB Nation.
