FAQ

How BasketKeep works.

What is BasketKeep?

BasketKeep is basketball on Ball Keep. The Keep is dynasty basketball, the full ranked tape, from 18 boards. The Board is this-year redraft. BK Value uses the same 12,000 curve as football.

How is The Keep ranked?

Average of every source that ranked the player.

Where is the NBA news?

BK News on this board clusters injury, roster, and coach tape. The banner on the news page says when the last scrape landed.
