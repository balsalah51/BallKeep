Market Tape · September 15, 2026

BK Hot 'n' Cold

After Week 1. Rising names to Buy and aging or overpriced names to Sell, pulled from the opener, ESPN, Reuters, the Associated Press, Sports Illustrated, RotoBaller, FantasyPros, and the recap tape.

Hot: Buy

1

Kenneth Walker RB KC

Associated Press after Monday: 173 yards and two scores in a Chiefs debut. Rooms that still have him as a Seahawk leftover will sell cheap. Pay the new city.

Source: Associated Press

2

Jalen Coker WR CAR

Sports Illustrated after Charlotte: eight of nine for 138 and two scores. Every major Week 2 waiver list has him first or second. Dynasty rooms can still buy the year-two leap.

Source: Sports Illustrated

3

Kaelon Black RB SF

Fourteen carries in Melbourne behind McCaffrey. ESPN and FantasyPros already treat him as the handcuff of the week. Get him before the next cart.

Source: ESPN, FantasyPros

4

Drake Maye QB NE

Three fourth-quarter picks in Seattle. The arm and the legs still play. Buy from the manager who watched Wednesday and closed the laptop.

Source: NFL Network

5

Puka Nacua WR LAR

Seven points on a Thursday in Australia. The target tree is still his. Week 2 is the Giants at home. Pay the ugly opener.

Source: The Recap

6

Devaughn Vele WR NO

Seven of nine for 69 and a score on 82 snaps next to Olave. RotoBaller and FantasyPros already have him on the wire. Stash the snap share.

Source: RotoBaller, FantasyPros

7

A.J. Brown WR NE

High-ankle sprain, boot, three-to-four weeks. IR him if the league allows it. Contenders can buy the missed month.

Source: NFL Network

8

George Kittle TE SF

Two catches, twelve yards, Achilles in the rearview. Shanahan metered him on purpose. That plan expires.

Source: The Athletic

9

TreVeyon Henderson RB NE

Missed the opener. The market already treated him like a ghost. The talent is still a Patriot backfield.

Source: Footballguys

10

Jaxon Smith-Njigba WR SEA

Worked after Lock entered. The target stays the target when the quarterback changes. Buy any dip that treats Wednesday like a lost season.

Source: The Athletic

Cold: Sell

1

D'Andre Swift RB CHI

Three scores and 124 yards in a 59-point game. Reuters called it a record. Someone in your league now believes he is a top-five back. Take the overpay.

Source: Reuters

2

David Montgomery RB HOU

Three scores in a loss to Buffalo. Houston will score again. The touchdown luck will calm down.

Source: CBS Sports

3

Kyle Monangai RB CHI

A 61-yard score on ten carries next to Swift. Fun tape. Fragile role. Sell the spike.

Source: Reuters

4

Tyler Shough QB NO

410 yards in a 21-0 hole. Superflex adds him. If a manager offers a real QB2 plus a pick, listen.

Source: ESPN

5

Carson Wentz QB MIN

A comeback in Green Bay already has people writing poems. The job is still a committee of circumstances.

Source: PFF

6

Cairo Santos K CHI

Every streamer list in the country just printed his name. If someone wants to trade a skill piece for a kicker, let them.

Source: RotoBaller

7

Brian Thomas Jr. WR JAX

Parker Washington still ate. Jacksonville won 34-10 and the target tree still looks crowded. Sell a name people drafted as a WR1.

Source: RotoWire

8

Josh Jacobs RB GB

The exemption is still the story. Green Bay lost 39-22 and the backfield stays messy. Take a 2027 second if it is still on the table.

Source: FantasyPros

9

Cam Ward QB TEN

The Titans scored ten against the Jets. Year 2 tape still asks for time. Flip after a spike week if you can.

Source: RotoWire

10

Tua Tagovailoa QB ATL

Oblique language after Pittsburgh. Atlanta still gets Carolina at home. The one-year deal already had a short window. Sell into hope.

Source: RotoBaller
