BK Hot 'n' Cold

Week 1 tape. Rising names to Buy and aging or overpriced names to Sell, pulled from FantasyPros risers and fallers (Sep 8), FantasyLife buy/sells and handcuff targets (Sep 8-9), Footballguys buy-the-dip (Sep 9), RotoWire Week 1 movers (Sep 9), and RotoBaller market watch.

Hot: Buy

Parker Washington WR JAX

RotoWire (Sep 9): Coen runs through the slot. Ranked with Higgins, Odunze, and Burden. Still acquirable while Jacksonville opens against a hard slate.

Source: RotoWire

2

De'Zhaun Stribling WR SF

FantasyPros (Sep 8): projected Niners slot next to Evans and Deebo. Dynasty WR41. A 2027 second plus a third is the ask.

Garrett Wilson WR NYJ

FantasyLife (Sep 8): 26, three 1,000-yard seasons already. Buy before the Week 1 reminder. Clean swap for Tate, or even a Nabers downgrade.

Source: FantasyLife

Oronde Gadsden II TE LAC

FantasyPros + RotoBaller: Njoku and Kolar stole camp reps. Still a TE10 long view. Mid-to-late second is the buy.

TreVeyon Henderson RB NE

Footballguys (Sep 9): ankle, out Week 1, market down to RB20. Buy the dip. Do not pay a future first.

Source: Footballguys

Javonte Williams RB DAL

FantasyPros: Jaydon Blue cut. RB12 last year on 35 catches. Contenders can pay a late 2027 first.

Source: FantasyPros

Kaleb Johnson RB GB

Source: FantasyLife

Kaelon Black RB SF

FantasyLife (Sep 9): Shanahan third-rounder behind CMC. If you roster McCaffrey, get Black now.

Source: FantasyLife

9

Malachi Fields WR NYG

Source: RotoWire

Chris Bell WR MIA

RotoWire: listed as a Dolphins starter. Biggest obstacle is Jalen Tolbert. Low-risk dart.
