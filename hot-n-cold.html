Hot: Buy

De'Zhaun Stribling WR SF

Jonah Coleman RB DEN

Kyle Pitts TE ATL

Zay Flowers WR BAL

Bryce Lance WR NO

Jelani Woods TE NYJ

Kyler Murray QB MIN

Ladd McConkey WR LAC

Jonathan Taylor RB IND
