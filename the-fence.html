Ball Keep's mixed Superflex + IDP dynasty Super Aggregate. Every mixed name the long boards ranked, skill and IDP on one board. The board IDP startups actually draft from.

How is the rank built?

Super Aggregate: 50% Glossery Mixed, the consensus stitch, The Keep, and Fence IDP, 50% every other mixed board that ranked the name.

How is this different from The Keep and Top Defenses?

The Keep is Superflex skill players only. Top Defenses is team DST. The Fence is the combined board: QB, RB, WR, TE, DL, LB, DB.
