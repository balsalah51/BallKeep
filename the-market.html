The Market

The opener moves prices. Buy the names whose rooms overreacted to one ugly night. Sell the names whose rooms just watched a carnival. This sits next to Hot 'n' Cold and Trade. Pictures live on the player pages. The recap lives on The Recap.

Buy low

Pay the dip.

WR · LAR

Puka Nacua

Seven points in Melbourne on a Thursday. The target tree is still his. Week 2 is the Giants at home. Buy the ugly opener while rooms are still staring at the 27-7.

QB · NE

Drake Maye

Three fourth-quarter picks in Seattle. The arm and the rushing still play. Pittsburgh visits in Week 2. Pay a discount, keep the long view.

WR · NE

A.J. Brown

High-ankle sprain, boot, three-to-four weeks. IR him if the league allows it. Contenders can buy the missed month.

RB · NE

TreVeyon Henderson

Missed the opener. The market already treated him like a ghost. The talent is still a Patriot backfield.

QB · LAR

Matthew Stafford

One half in Australia. Davante and Puka are still in the building. Monday night against the Giants is a cleaner stage.

TE · SF

George Kittle

Two catches, twelve yards, Achilles in the rearview. Shanahan metered him. That is a plan, and plans expire.

RB · LAR

Kyren Williams

The Melbourne box score is cold. The role is the same. Buy from someone who watched Thursday and closed the tab.

QB · SEA

Sam Darnold

Hip, out in Arizona. Lock starts. Superflex rooms can buy the weeks after the MRI.

Sell high

Take the overpay.

RB · CHI

D'Andre Swift

Three scores and 124 yards in a 59-point game. Someone in your league now believes he is a top-five back. Take the overpay.

QB · NO

Tyler Shough

410 yards in a 21-0 hole. Superflex adds him. If a manager offers a real QB2 plus a pick, listen.

RB · CHI

Kyle Monangai

A 61-yard score on ten carries next to Swift. Fun tape. Fragile role. Sell the spike.

QB · MIN

Carson Wentz

A comeback in Green Bay already has people writing poems. The job is still a committee of circumstances.

RB · HOU

David Montgomery

Three scores in a loss to Buffalo. Houston will score again. The touchdown luck will calm down.
